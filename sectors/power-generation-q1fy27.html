08 / Red Flags & Accounting Forensics

State Discom Delay & Overdue Receivables

State electricity distribution companies (Discoms) accumulating regulatory asset overdues can strain power generator cash flows.

Imported Coal & LNG Tariff Volatility

Unhedged merchant power generation relying on imported coal or spot LNG faces margin squeeze if international fuel prices spike.

Thermal Plant Environmental Compliance Capex

Mandatory Flue Gas Desulfurization (FGD) retrofits on aging coal plants require ongoing capital expenditure.

10 / Sector Synthesis & Strategic Outlook

India's Power Generation sector is undergoing a massive dual growth phase: base-load thermal power plants running at maximum capacity while utility renewable energy (solar, wind, hydro, BESS) scales exponentially.

Power utilities with guaranteed regulated equity returns (NTPC) and green transition leaders (Tata Power, JSW Energy) offer superior long-term institutional returns.
