Sector earnings review · Quarter ended 30 September 2026

Cables & Wires
Q2 FY27

Record revenue, and most of it is copper. Copper rose 40–45% in H1, so the leaders reported value growth of 35–65% on volume growth of mid-single digits to about 10%. Margins held at the leaders but slipped at KEI, Havells cables and Universal. Working capital absorbed the copper: inventory rose by ₹1,200 cr at KEI and ₹460 cr at Finolex. The market has stopped paying for the growth. KEI and RR Kabel are down 29% in three months, as UltraTech and other new entrants threaten the margins the P&L does not yet show falling.

The new-entrant discount

Share price fall over three months to 13 Nov 2026, %

The entrantsJM Financial (2 Sep) sized UltraTech and Diamond Power at ~₹17,000 cr of revenue by FY29, about 11–12% of the market. It downgraded Polycab and KEI to Add and RR Kabel to Reduce. Havells named Crompton among the new wire entrants and expects "some" margin compromise for a while.
The leaders' answerPolycab called UltraTech's impact "negligible", said it will not cut prices, and will keep its premium. It disputes channel checks showing rivals 10–15% cheaper. KEI's dealer count fell to 2,146 from ~2,340, which management calls normal churn.
What the P&L shows so farHavells cables contribution margin fell 380 bps YoY, which it blamed on aluminium. KEI's gross margin fell ~100 bps against +50 bps guided. Polycab's EBITDA margin fell ~1.4 pts YoY*, though it is still above its own 11–13% long-term band. The damage is early and partial. The price is already discounting more.

What management said

From the Q2 FY27 calls and results. Grouped by theme.

Copper and volume
  • Polycab: copper rose 40–45% in H1 but only ~6% QoQ in Q2. Pass-through takes ~1 month for cables and ~2 weeks for wires.
  • Polycab: H1 volumes grew mid-to-high single digits. It guides W&C growth of 12–13% for FY27.
  • KEI: rising copper cuts volume but lifts value. It guides FY28 volume growth of 18–20% if copper stays flat.
  • Havells: cables and wires volume close to double digits. Cables margin was hit by aluminium.
Margin guidance
  • Polycab: W&C EBITDA margin 11–13% long term. H1 ran ~15%, partly on copper.
  • KEI: 11–12% reiterated, with FY27 ~11%. The CFO said there was "no guidance cut".
  • RR Kabel: FY27 EBITDA margin 9.5%. That needs ~10.2% in H2, above any recent quarter.
  • Finolex: communication margin normalises to 8–9% by Q3, from 30% in Q2.
  • Apar: cables 11% within its 10–12% band. The oils windfall may not recur, and it gives no guide.
Competition
  • Polycab: UltraTech is a "negligible impact", and it will not cut prices.
  • Havells: new wire entrants could cause a "slight compromise" on margins.
  • KEI: the share fall on results day was linked to UltraTech's entry. Its active dealers fell ~8% YoY.
  • JM Financial: industry volume growth has stalled, and C&W trades about 25% above its long-term P/E.
Working capital
  • KEI: inventory rose ₹1,200 cr in H1 (₹600–700 cr Sanand stocking, the rest copper). It targets 90 days of COGS by March.
  • Polycab: working capital 11 days with LC support. Management expects it to normalise to 45–50 days.
  • Finolex: H1 operating cash flow −₹106 cr and inventory +₹460 cr.
  • RR Kabel: H1 CFO/PAT 0.32x, FCF −₹159 cr, and net debt tripled to ₹447 cr.
Capacity
  • KEI: Sanand Phase 2 (EHV/HT) starts commercial production by March 2027, adding ₹1,500–1,700 cr of EHV capacity.
  • Polycab: EHV plant commissioning in FY28. H1 capex ₹610 cr.
  • Havells: ~₹1,400 cr of FY28 capex, mostly cables and wires. The full cables expansion takes ~18 months.
  • RR Kabel: Silvassa Unit 3 (12,000 tpa) started 15 Sep. Finolex: its fiber milestone slipped.
Exports
  • Polycab: exports +85% QoQ, but only 5% of revenue (6.5% in FY26).
  • KEI: exports flat in H1 (₹834 cr against ₹846 cr). The 25% full-year export ambition needs a steep H2.
  • Apar: US revenue −30% under Section 232 tariffs. It booked a ₹43 cr tariff refund.
  • Universal Cables: exports ₹138 cr, +139%. Paramount: export order book ₹99 cr.

The call

Own now

Polycab

Polycab has the highest margin (14.4%) and the scale to hold its price premium. FMEG turned double-digit at EBIT for the first time. The stock is up only 3.6% in a year and down 15% in three months, so the entrant risk is partly priced. The margin will drift toward 11–13% as copper normalises, so own it for the share, not for the margin.

Accumulate on evidence

KEI · Havells · Apar · V-Guard · Finolex · Dynamic · Paramount

KEI needs H2 margin ≥11% and Sanand on time by March. Havells needs its cables contribution margin back toward 15% after the Q3 price increases. Apar needs H2 conductor volume of about +25% to reach its guide. V-Guard needs its gross margin back toward 36.9%. Finolex needs operating cash flow to turn positive as communication margins normalise. Dynamic (order book ₹829 cr) and Paramount are small; wait for H2 plant and export delivery.

Stay out

RR Kabel · Universal Cables · Precision Wires · Ultracab

RR Kabel combines negative free cash flow, tripled net debt, FMEG losses and a margin guide that needs a record H2. Universal's EBITDA grew at half its revenue. Precision Wires earns ~5% margins and is issuing CCDs. Ultracab's Q2 is undisclosed and its profit is falling. The market already agrees on RR Kabel (−29% in 3M), but it is still +44% over a year.

What would make us more positive
  • Q3 volume growth back into double digits at Polycab and KEI as copper stabilises.
  • Evidence UltraTech is pricing at par, not 10–15% below, in its first full quarter.
  • Inventory unwinding: KEI to 90 days of COGS by March, Finolex OCF positive.
What would make us cut
  • Leader wire margins down another 100 bps+ QoQ in Q3.
  • A copper fall of 15%+, which deflates revenue and creates inventory losses on the high-cost stock.
  • Dealer attrition accelerating at KEI or RR Kabel.
