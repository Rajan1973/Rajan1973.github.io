Spread — the league table

Spread = (revenue − finance cost) / revenue, derived from Tijori. It is not NIM: it includes fee income and is measured on revenue, not assets. Use it for direction, not level. It widened QoQ only at Shriram, Muthoot and Manappuram; the other four narrowed.

Caveats

  • Universe. Not in the skill's reference file; built from the seven largest listed vehicle and gold lenders. Muthoot Microfin and smaller gold lenders are excluded.
  • Format. Tijori files these NBFCs in a corporate format. "Net revenue" (revenue − finance cost) includes fee income and is not NII; Shriram's company NII of ₹8,410 cr compares with Tijori-derived ₹8,827 cr. Opex includes credit cost.
  • Profit basis. Sundaram is PAT before associates; IIFL is after minority interest; Muthoot is consolidated.
  • Sources. Filed quarterlies from Tijori; Mahindra Finance's call transcript (NSE); Free Press Journal (Muthoot, IIFL); SAHI (Shriram guidance); gold-rate reports for the price context. AI digests were used only as leads. No transcript was read for Shriram, Chola, Sundaram, Muthoot or Manappuram.
  • Rate hike. The hike is as described by Mahindra's management; its date and size were not verified.
  • Dates. Results dates marked n/s were not verified.
