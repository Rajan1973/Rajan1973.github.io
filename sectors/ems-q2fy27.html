Sector earnings review · Quarter ended 30 September 2026

Electronics Manufacturing Services
Q2 FY27

Revenue is growing and cash is not. Seven of ten EMS companies grew revenue more than 35%, but almost every one funded it with inventory and debt. H1 free cash flow was negative at Amber (−₹1,576 cr), Syrma, PG Electroplast and Avalon, and Kaynes' operating cash flow was −₹278 cr. Memory and component inflation compressed margins at Dixon, Syrma, Amber and Elin. Kaynes is the outlier for the wrong reason: revenue +4%, consolidated PAT −66%, and net debt doubled in six months. Its results came out on a Saturday, so the price in this report has not reacted yet.

What management said

From the Q2 FY27 calls and results. Kaynes has not held its call yet; its row uses the filing only.

Memory and input costs
  • Dixon: memory inflation compresses margins, and ODM pass-through lags. It does not expect revenue to fall even if memory normalises.
  • Syrma: material ratio 78.9% (75.5% in Q1) on memory, laminate and copper.
  • Elin: polymer and metal inflation plus the rupee; it cut low-priced batten orders to protect margin.
  • PG Electroplast: material 82.3% of revenue, ~390 bps worse YoY. October 1 price increases must hold.
Volumes and demand
  • Dixon: 9.7 mn phones in Q2. Q3 guided at 7–7.5 mn, FY27 33–34 mn and FY28 55–60 mn including Vivo.
  • Dixon cites India's smartphone market down ~26% YoY in Q2 (industry reports).
  • Cyient DLM: record order book ₹2,814 cr, book-to-bill 1.6x. Aerospace revenue +62%.
  • Avalon: FY27 growth guide raised to 26–30% (from 24–27%). ~62% of revenue from US customers.
Guidance
  • Syrma: FY27 growth above 35% (raised). EBITDA ₹700 cr and a 10.5–11% margin.
  • PG Electroplast: 25–30% growth and ~8% EBITDA ex-PLI. It promised to beat FY25 PAT (₹291 cr).
  • Amber: electronics growth ~40%, mobility margin 15–16%, and FY27 capex ₹1,800–2,000 cr.
  • Dixon: FY27 capex ~₹1,200 cr. IT hardware revenue ~₹6,000 cr in FY27.
New capacity
  • Dixon: HKC notebook line in production by Q4 FY27, mobile line early FY28. Display fab mass production from FY28. Vivo JV from Q4 FY27.
  • Kaynes: first OSAT/PCB billings were guided for Q3. CWIP rose ₹334 cr QoQ to ₹706 cr.
  • PG Electroplast: compressor and refrigerator capacity from December or January.
  • EPACK: revenue doubled, but the loss widened on new-capacity fixed costs.
Working capital promises
  • Dixon: 80–85% of the blocked working capital released by March 2027.
  • Kaynes: guided 70–85 NWC days by March 2026. CompoundingAI estimates ~257 days now.
  • Avalon: 120–130 NWC days guided. Its derived proxy is ~124 days.
  • PG Electroplast: inventory below ₹900 cr was promised on the Q4 call. It is ₹1,353 cr.
One-offs
  • Cyient DLM: reported PAT ₹34 cr includes an ₹18.2 cr earnout reversal. Normalised PAT ₹15.8 cr.
  • Dixon: EBITDA and PAT exclude a fair-value gain on Aditya Infotech.
  • Amber: an inventory drawdown swing of ₹94.6 cr and a fire at IL JIN Noida (₹56.5 cr claim).
  • Kaynes: standalone PAT +296%, but nine unreviewed subsidiaries lost ₹54 cr.

The call

Own, sized for the cash risk

Syrma SGS · Avalon

Both raised growth guidance and delivered above it. Syrma grew 74% with a margin in its band and net cash of ₹253 cr. Avalon expanded its margin 246 bps with gross margin inside its 33–35% band. Neither generated cash in H1, and both are up 68–95% in a year. Hold them for execution and cut them if Q3 inventory does not turn.

Accumulate on evidence

Dixon · Cyient DLM · PG Electroplast · Centum

Dixon needs Q3 phone volumes of 7–7.5 mn without further margin loss, and its working capital back by March. Cyient DLM needs to convert its record ₹2,814 cr order book at better than an 8.7% margin. PG Electroplast needs the October price increases to hold and inventory back toward ₹900 cr. Centum needs to disclose its margins.

Stay out

Kaynes · Amber · Elin · EPACK

Kaynes combines stalled revenue, a −66% consolidated profit, doubled net debt and working capital far above its promise, with no call yet to explain it. Amber is loss-making with D/E of 0.73x and −₹1,576 cr H1 FCF. Elin cannot pass through costs. EPACK's losses widened. The market already agrees on Kaynes (−46%), Elin (−59%) and EPACK (−44%) over a year. Amber is +15.5% this month despite its results.

What would make us more positive
  • Memory prices peaking, so Dixon and Syrma's material ratios fall back toward 75%.
  • Q3 operating cash flow positive at Syrma, Avalon and PG Electroplast as inventory unwinds.
  • Kaynes' call (expected the week of 18 Nov) explaining the subsidiary losses and showing OSAT billings.
What would make us cut
  • Another quarter of rising net debt without margin recovery at Amber or Kaynes.
  • Indian smartphone volumes still falling 20%+ YoY into Q3, which would hit Dixon's mobile base.
  • A memory price correction that forces inventory write-downs on the strategic stock.
