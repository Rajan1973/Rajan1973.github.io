What management said

From the Q2 FY27 calls and results. Kaynes items come from its 15–16 Nov call coverage and press release; the other companies' items come from call digests and were not re-read against transcripts.

Memory and input costs
  • Dixon: memory inflation compresses margins, and ODM pass-through lags. It does not expect revenue to fall even if memory normalises.
  • Syrma: material and traded-goods cost 78.9% of revenue (75.5% in Q1) on memory, laminate and copper.
  • Elin: polymer and metal inflation plus the rupee; it cut low-priced batten orders to protect margin.
  • PG Electroplast: October 1 price increases must hold.
Volumes and demand
  • Dixon: 9.7 mn phones in Q2. Q3 guided at 7–7.5 mn, FY27 33–34 mn and FY28 55–60 mn including Vivo.
  • Kaynes: core EMS +34% to ~₹799 cr; smart-meter revenue −80% to ~₹54 cr, held back deliberately.
  • Cyient DLM: record order book ₹2,814 cr, book-to-bill 1.6x. Aerospace revenue +62%.
  • Avalon: FY27 growth guide raised to 26–30% (from 24–27%).
Guidance
  • Syrma: FY27 growth above 35% (raised). EBITDA ₹700 cr and a 10.5–11% margin.
  • Kaynes: electronics growth above 30% in FY27. Product share of EMS to rise from ~8% toward 32%. No QIP in the next 12 months.
  • PG Electroplast: 25–30% growth and ~8% EBITDA ex-PLI. It promised to beat FY25 PAT (₹291 cr).
  • Amber: electronics growth ~40%, mobility margin 15–16%, and FY27 capex ₹1,800–2,000 cr.
New capacity
  • Dixon: HKC notebook line by Q4 FY27, mobile line early FY28. Display fab mass production from FY28. Vivo JV from Q4 FY27.
  • Kaynes: OSAT and PCB unit 2 operational and in customer validation. PCB unit 1 revenue from Q3; both at full run-rate by Q4. Q2 capex ~₹221 cr before subsidy.
  • Kaynes: Vishay to take 49% of Kaynes Semicon and 75–90% of its capacity; definitive agreement expected in 2–3 months.
  • PG Electroplast: compressor and refrigerator capacity from December or January.
Working capital promises
  • Dixon: 80–85% of the blocked working capital released by March 2027.
  • Kaynes: 169 days (rolling 12 months), up from 123 at Q1, against 70–85 days promised. It aims to flatten, then reduce, and will review ₹200–250 cr of strategic inventory by end-December.
  • Kaynes: smart-meter receivables ₹1,091 cr (₹1,402 cr at Q1); production resumes only below ₹750 cr.
  • PG Electroplast: inventory below ₹900 cr was promised on the Q4 call. It is ₹1,353 cr.
One-offs and accounting
  • Kaynes: prior periods restated under Ind AS 8 (revenue timing, capitalised overheads in inventory, receivable provisions, bill discounting moved into borrowings). FY26 standalone PAT fell to ~₹182 cr from ~₹254 cr.
  • Kaynes: qualified review conclusion on the consolidated results, because associate Mixx Technologies' figures were not reviewed. Share of associate loss ₹6.0 cr.
  • Cyient DLM: reported PAT ₹34 cr includes an ~₹18 cr earnout reversal. Normalised PAT ~₹16 cr.
  • Dixon: the YoY PAT fall is mostly a ~₹490 cr other-income gain in the base quarter.

The call

Own, sized for the cash risk

Syrma SGS · Avalon

Both raised growth guidance and delivered above it. Syrma grew 74% with PAT +87%. Avalon expanded its margin 247 bps with gross margin inside its band. Both borrowed to fund inventory, and Syrma's margin sits below its band before other income. Avalon's 12% post-result fall improves the entry price. Cut either if Q3 debt keeps rising without margin recovery.

Accumulate on evidence

Dixon · Cyient DLM · PG Electroplast · Centum

Dixon needs Q3 phone volumes of 7–7.5 mn without further margin loss, and its working capital back by March. Cyient DLM needs to convert its record ₹2,814 cr order book at better than an 8.7% margin. PG Electroplast needs the October price increases to hold and inventory back toward ₹900 cr. Centum needs to update its order book.

Stay out

Kaynes · Amber · Elin · EPACK

Kaynes' call explained the smart-meter drag, but the results added a qualified review and a restatement, and working capital is at 169 days. A cheaper price does not fix an accounting question. Amber is loss-making, with debt up ₹1,586 cr in six months. Elin cannot pass through costs. EPACK's losses widened. Over a year: Kaynes −55%, Elin −61%, EPACK −48%, Amber −15%.

What would make us more positive
  • Memory prices peaking, so Dixon and Syrma's material ratios fall back toward 75%.
  • Gross debt flat or lower at the December quarter for Syrma, Avalon and PG Electroplast as inventory unwinds.
  • Kaynes: an unqualified review, the Vishay definitive agreement signed, and working capital falling below 150 days.
What would make us cut
  • Another quarter of rising debt without margin recovery at Amber or Kaynes.
  • Indian smartphone volumes still falling 20%+ YoY into Q3, which would hit Dixon's mobile base.
  • A memory price correction that forces write-downs on strategic stock at Dixon or Syrma.
