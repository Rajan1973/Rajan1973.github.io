The call

Own now

Supreme Industries · Kajaria Ceramics

Supreme grew volume 19% and held its margin through the polymer spike; it is −11% in a year. Kajaria has the highest margin and is still widening it.

Accumulate on evidence

Astral · Prince Pipes · Century Plyboards

Astral's volume is strong; Q3 must prove pricing power. Prince's recovery is two quarters old. Century Ply's capacity ramp is working, but the interest bill is rising.

Stay out

Finolex Industries · Apollo Pipes

Finolex is the most resin-exposed and leans on other income. Apollo barely breaks even, and its price has run far ahead of its numbers.

What would make us more positive
  • Astral's Q3 plumbing margin back above 18%.
  • PVC prices stabilising under the import floor.
What would make us cut
  • Supreme's Q3 volume growth falling back below 10%.
  • Gas price increases hitting Kajaria's margin.
