What management said

From the Q2 FY27 calls, releases and presentations. Grouped by theme.

Demand
  • Birlasoft: demand is "steady, not worsening, but not improving either".
  • LTTS expects revenue momentum to strengthen in H2 FY27.
  • Tata Technologies is confident of "strong double-digit growth" for FY27.
  • Mastek and Tata Elxsi both expect a better H2.
Deals and AI
  • Coforge: $1.04 bn of orders with eight large deals. Executable order book $2.38 bn, +45%.
  • Mphasis: TCV $466 mn, 66% of it AI-led.
  • LTTS: over $200 mn in large deals, more than half AI-related.
  • Birlasoft says about a third of its code is now written with agentic AI tools.
Margins and wages
  • Coforge raised FY27 EBIT guidance from 15.5% to 16%, and targets 17% by Q4.
  • Birlasoft absorbed a 170 bps wage hit and held 15.2%.
  • Mastek lost about 100 bps to salary hikes and ESOPs.
  • Tata Elxsi expanded EBITDA 70 bps QoQ despite salary revisions.
Acquisitions
  • Persistent has secured 94.04% of Nagarro. Up to $450 mn of equity is approved, with equity and debt capped at $1.25 bn.
  • Coforge: first full quarter of Encora.
  • Cyient: one month of TAO Digital, plus a second quarter of Kinetic.
  • Happiest Minds: the ITC Infotech merger is "progressing well". It targets $1 bn revenue by FY28.
Client concentration
  • KPIT: revenue from its two largest accounts fell about 13% YoY. It expects that to bottom out over the next couple of quarters.
  • Birlasoft: an Energy & Utilities client ramped down, with a turnaround expected in 2–3 quarters.
  • Mastek: healthcare project starts were delayed and its backlog fell QoQ.
Cash and longer-term targets
  • Birlasoft: cash of $300 mn, DSO 51 days, its best in two years.
  • LTTS: aims for a 13–15% revenue CAGR and 16–17% EBIT margin over five years.
  • Cyient: record Q2 order intake of $158 mn, +21%.

Red flags

Ranked by how much they should change position sizing, not by how alarming they sound.

Act on these
KPIT
Dollar revenue fell 2.2% YoY and constant-currency growth was 0.2%. Its two largest accounts are down about 13%. Qorix losses rose to ₹15.6 cr and there was an FX loss of ₹17.1 cr. All of its rupee growth is currency.
Persistent
EBIT rose 19.5% YoY while PAT fell 25.4%, and the release gives no reason. Nagarro is a funded transformation: up to $450 mn of equity and borrowings of up to $1.25 bn in total. From Q3, organic growth gets hard to see.
Coforge
Rupee revenue +53.6% YoY is mostly Encora. Organic growth was 3.8% QoQ in constant currency, and the quarter missed some estimates. The Q1 finding, a headline that masks low organic growth, still holds.
Monitor next quarter
Cyient
Headline cc growth of 6.3% YoY. Organic was 0.4%. TAO Digital and Kinetic are carrying the number.
Mastek
The 12-month backlog fell QoQ on deferred starts. EBITDA margin was down 100 bps.
Sonata
Sources conflict on international growth (19.0% vs 10.1% YoY), and the reported −33.9% QoQ looks like a data error. No call transcript was published.
Tata Technologies
EBIT margin slipped to 12.9% from 13.3% QoQ. Sources disagree on PAT YoY (+4% vs −5%). Rupee growth of 28% is not split into organic and acquired.
Note and move on
LTTS
PAT +67% includes a ₹165 cr gain on the SWC sale. Excluding it, PAT grew about 17% (our calculation).
Hexaware
Adjusted EBIT margin was down 88 bps YoY and cc growth only 4.5%. It reports on a calendar year, so the quarters don't line up with peers.
Happiest Minds
Dollar growth was only 3.6% YoY against 12.5% in rupees. Reported PAT was not disclosed; only the adjusted figure, excluding merger costs.
Sector-wide
A rupee about 7% weaker flatters every rupee number in this report. If it reverses, so do the rupee growth rates.

The call

Own now

Mphasis

Constant-currency growth of 9.7% YoY with no acquisition in the number. Direct business grew 11.3% and 66% of TCV is AI-led. Margin is within 50 bps of last year. It is the cleanest organic growth in the group.

Accumulate on evidence

Coforge · Persistent · Cyient

All three have strong order books. In each, growth is increasingly acquired: Encora, Nagarro and TAO Digital. Buy when organic growth is disclosed and holding: Coforge at 3% or more QoQ organic, Persistent with Nagarro funded without heavy dilution, Cyient with organic growth above 2%.

Stay out

KPIT · Sonata

KPIT's dollar revenue is shrinking and its two largest accounts are down 13%. The rupee is the only thing growing. Sonata's disclosure is too inconsistent this quarter to underwrite.

What would make us more positive
  • Median dollar growth above 8% YoY, so growth stops depending on the rupee.
  • TCV converting: the Q3 revenue run-rate catching up with H1 order books at Coforge, Persistent and Mphasis.
  • KPIT's top-two account decline bottoming by Q4, as management guides.
What would make us cut
  • The rupee strengthening back toward ₹90/$, which takes away most of the 9-point rupee tailwind.
  • Persistent funding Nagarro at the full $450 mn of equity.
  • Coforge organic growth below 2% QoQ.

Caveats

  • All 14 tracked companies have reported. Mphasis and Sonata were the last, on 5 November.
  • Margin bases differ. EBIT for Persistent, Coforge, LTTS, Cyient and Tata Technologies. Adjusted EBIT for Hexaware. Operating margin for Mphasis and Happiest Minds. EBITDA for the rest. They sit in separate league tables for that reason.
  • YoY constant-currency growth is disclosed by only 7 of 14. The others give QoQ cc growth or rupee growth only. The scissor covers five names with comparable growth and margin-change data.
  • Several margin changes are derived. Persistent (−1.4 pts) and Happiest Minds (+1.5 pts) are worked out from reported EBIT or operating-profit growth against revenue growth.
  • Acquisitions distort YoY comparisons. Coforge (Encora), Cyient (TAO Digital, Kinetic) and possibly Tata Technologies. Persistent's Nagarro deal had not closed by quarter-end.
  • Source conflicts are flagged, not resolved. These cover Sonata's international growth, Tata Technologies' and Tata Elxsi's PAT YoY, and Cyient's rupee growth (15.7% in one source, 25% in another).
  • The consensus comparison is a Q1-based outlook, Anand Rathi's of 24 August 2026, not a Q2 preview or post-result poll.
  • The rupee rate is one source's read (₹88.8 to ₹95 per dollar), quoted in a cement company's commentary.
