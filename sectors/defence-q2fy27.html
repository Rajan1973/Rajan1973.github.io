The pressure point: PSU execution margins

Op. margin change YoY, pts

What brokeMazagon Dock took "higher provisions" and its margin fell to 8.1% from 23.7%. BDL billed old work-in-progress (inventory −₹945 cr) at a 6.1% margin. Cochin's margin fell to 4.1% from 17.7% in Q1.
What hid itOther income: 68% of BDL's PBT, 63% of Mazagon's, 40% of HAL's, and more than all of Cochin's. Headline PAT falls far less than operating profit.
Who did not breakSolar (+2.0 pts), Astra (+11.1) and Paras (+1.4) widened margins on mix. GRSE (+1.4) was the only shipyard to improve.

Order books turn into revenue. Eleven of thirteen grew revenue; BDL +45%, BEML +42%, Solar +69%.

Revenue brings margin. At PSU shipyards and BDL, revenue came at lower margins, and other income propped up PAT.

The call

Own now

Solar Industries · Astra Microwave

Solar grew operating profit 82% with almost no other income and expects to beat its guide. Astra added 11 pts of margin. Both have run (+39% and +71% in a year), but both are flat to down over three months.

Accumulate on evidence

BEL · GRSE · Data Patterns · HAL

BEL runs a 28% margin, earns from operations, and is −6% in a year. GRSE is the only shipyard improving. Data Patterns needs H2 revenue to match its inflow. HAL needs the Tejas ramp to show in margins.

Stay out

Mazagon Dock · BDL · Cochin Shipyard · BEML

Their margins fell 2–16 pts and other income now carries their profit. Down 32–36% in a year, but not yet cheap on core earnings.

What would make us more positive
  • Mazagon discloses the provision as one-off and Q3 margin returns above 15%.
  • BDL's margin back in double digits as new orders replace old WIP.
  • HAL's Q3 margin back above 25%.
What would make us cut
  • Solar or Astra margins falling back as mix normalises.
  • Data Patterns' H2 revenue failing to recover.
