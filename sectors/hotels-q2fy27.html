The call

Own now

ITC Hotels · IHCL

ITC Hotels grew operating profit 37% with no debt and is −17% in a year. IHCL raised its guide and has the pipeline; it is the one stock already rewarded.

Accumulate on evidence

Lemon Tree · EIH · SAMHI

Lemon Tree's occupancy and rates both rose. EIH widened its margin but its interest jumped. SAMHI is deleveraging but has not expanded its margin.

Stay out

Ventive · Juniper · Chalet

Ventive's profit more than halved. Juniper's operating profit was flat. Chalet's quarter cannot be read without the hospitality segment.

What would make us more positive
  • Ventive's depreciation stabilising and its tax rate normalising.
  • Chalet's hospitality-only margin above 42%.
What would make us cut
  • IHCL's Q3 RevPAR growth below 15%.
  • New supply in key metros pushing rates down.

Caveats

  • Sources. All financials are consolidated filed quarterlies from Tijori, with operating profit before other income. Commentary comes from IHCL's and Lemon Tree's releases as reported by Asian Hospitality. Tijori had no Q2 transcripts for this set by 15 November.
  • Tijori against management.
    • IHCL: 30.7% EBITDA margin is on total income including other income: (₹706 cr + ₹82 cr) / ₹2,566 cr. Tijori's 28.4% excludes other income. They reconcile.

  • Core metric. RevPAR, occupancy and ARR were verified only for IHCL and Lemon Tree. The scissor uses revenue as a stand-in. This is the weakest part of the report.
