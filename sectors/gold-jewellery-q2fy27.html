Sector earnings review · Quarter ended 30 September 2026

Gold Jewellery Retail
Q2 FY27

The price did the work, and the duty hike flattered the margins. Gold was up ~47% YoY and the customs duty rose from 6% to 15% in May, which revalued every jeweller's inventory. Titan booked ₹536 cr of customs-duty gains in Q2, and its buyer count was flat. Strip the one-offs and the picture splits. PN Gadgil, Goldiam and Kalyan grew underlying profit. Thangamayil's normalised PAT fell 35% and Senco's EBITDA fell 15%. The market has already paid for the winners: Kalyan rose 40% this month alone.

The duty windfallCustoms duty rose from 6% to 15% in mid-May 2026, which revalued inventory already held. Titan booked ₹407 cr in Q1 and ₹536 cr in Q2. Senco booked ₹30 cr, Thangamayil ₹28.5 cr, and Kalyan's adjusted EBITDA is ₹51 cr below reported. The gains run off as the old stock sells through Q3.
The volume truthTitan's buyers grew 0%. Thangamayil's gold volume fell 1% and its silver volume 28%. WGC put Apr–Jun Indian jewellery volume at −15%. Kalyan's exchange mix rose to ~42% from ~30%, so customers are trading in old gold rather than buying new.
The competitionTitan says rivals are funding discounts out of their duty gains into Q4. It also cites grey-channel gold at a 5–6% discount and lab-grown entrants. Titan reiterated its 11% FY27 jewellery margin. Its adjusted Q2 was 11.1%, so it has no room to spare.

What management said

From the Q2 FY27 calls, presentations and releases. Grouped by theme.

Demand
  • Titan: buyer growth 0% (5% in Q1). Entry-level demand is muted at these gold prices. October sales fell YoY on a base that is not like-for-like.
  • Kalyan: SSSG ~20%. Weddings are delayed 15–20 days by price volatility, not cancelled.
  • PN Gadgil: SSSG 25.5%. About ₹200 cr of revenue shifted from Q2 into Q3 with Navratri.
  • Senco: SSSG 30% per results coverage, against 19% in its 8 Oct business update.
Margins and guidance
  • Titan: FY27 jewellery margin 11% reiterated. Hedging MTM swings of 40–60 bps are now "part and parcel".
  • Senco: H2 EBITDA margin 7.5–7.8% and 25% growth, from a 4.4% Q2.
  • Kalyan: H2 PBT margin better than H1. Revenue growth of at least 25% for FY27.
  • Sky Gold: 7.94%, above its 7.0–7.5% guide. Exports 18% of revenue.
Duty and hedging
  • Titan: customs gains ₹536 cr in Q2 and ₹943 cr in H1. It hedges conservatively and forgoes unhedged gains.
  • Thangamayil: duty-hike inventory gain of ₹28.5 cr (₹59 cr H1), in line with its ~₹60 cr guide.
  • Senco: customs gain ₹30 cr (₹16 cr in Q1). Hedging at 40–50% was cited as a margin pressure.
  • Kalyan: adjusted figures strip a one-time customs benefit. It is debt-free on non-GML debt.
Competition
  • Titan: rivals are funding discounts with their duty gains, and expects this into Q4.
  • Titan: smuggled gold at a 5–6% discount, and lab-grown diamond entrants rising.
  • Kalyan: no confirmation that making-charge discounting has eased.
  • CaratLane: EBIT margin 6.5% from 10.2% on deliberate brand investment.
Expansion
  • Senco: 215 stores (123 own, 92 franchise). 10–12 more in H2.
  • PN Gadgil: ~23 new stores in FY27, mostly in H2.
  • Kalyan: four new showrooms planned in H2.
  • Thangamayil: 4 of 9 planned showrooms opened in H1.
Capital
  • Titan: a ₹2,500 cr board-approved raise, described as refinancing.
  • Sky Gold: QIP of up to ₹1,800 cr, after saying no dilution was planned. Net debt ₹874 cr.
  • Thangamayil: net debt up ₹590 cr since March to ₹1,034 cr. H1 OCF −₹542 cr.
  • RBZ: finance cost +117% YoY.

The call

Own now

PN Gadgil · Goldiam

PN Gadgil grew adjusted EBITDA 60% at an 8.2% adjusted margin, with SSSG of 25.5% and ₹200 cr of festive revenue moved into Q3. It is −3% over a year, so the market has not paid for it. Goldiam earns a 25% margin as a lab-grown exporter, with no gold-duty exposure, and has fallen 14% in three months.

Accumulate on evidence

Titan · Kalyan · Senco

Titan's adjusted margin is on its 11% guide, but buyers were flat and the duty gains run off in Q3. Wait for buyer growth to return. Kalyan's underlying PAT grew 28%, but the stock rose 40% this month, so wait for a pullback or a clean Q3. Senco has to deliver its 7.5–7.8% H2 margin guide from 4.4% before it earns a position.

Stay out

Thangamayil · Sky Gold · RBZ · Motisons · Vaibhav · Utssav

Thangamayil's normalised profit fell 35% while its debt grew ₹590 cr. Sky Gold broke its deleveraging and no-dilution promises. RBZ and Motisons lost 640 bps and ~15 pts of margin. Vaibhav's EBITDA fell. Utssav's H1 was undisclosed. The market disagrees on two: Sky Gold is +195% and Utssav +185% over a year. That money is chasing growth that is being financed by debt and dilution.

What would make us more positive
  • Titan buyer growth back to 5%+ in Q3 as gold growth falls to single digits.
  • Adjusted Q3 margins holding after the duty gains run off.
  • A gold correction of 10%+ that revives entry-level and wedding volume.
What would make us cut
  • A customs duty cut back toward 6%, which reverses the inventory gains into losses.
  • Discounting persisting into Q4 as rivals spend their duty gains.
  • Rising debt at the regional chains if gold stays high and inventory keeps building.

Caveats

  • Research route. The Tapetide transcript service was capped for the day. Figures come from third-party, AI-assisted digests of filings and calls (CompoundingAI/Cofacto), Multibagg, Univest, Sahi and Upstox, so they are one step removed from the filing.
  • Reported vs adjusted. Titan, Kalyan, Thangamayil and Senco carry customs-duty or inventory gains in Q2. We show reported figures in the charts and adjusted figures in the cards. Titan's EBITDA growth (~+59%*) is derived from its reported margin of 14.0% (+299 bps).
  • Kalyan's consolidated revenue YoY is not available in sources we reached. The scissor uses India revenue (+32%) and India EBITDA (+27%).
  • Senco SSSG conflict: 19% in the 8 Oct business update, against 30% in results coverage on 14 Nov. Its YoY EBITDA margin (~6.9%*) is derived from −15% EBITDA growth.
  • Other derived figures:
    • Thangamayil's EBITDA growth (~+7%*) is derived from its margin on retail sales.
    • Sky Gold's EBITDA growth (~+90%*) is derived from its margin.
    • Vaibhav's YoY margin (~10.0%*) is derived.
  • Prices. Utssav is NSE SME (UTSSAV-ST) and RBZ trades in the BE segment. Their prices are thinly traded.
  • Universe. The tracker carries 11 names (the Q1 report had 12). Utssav reports half-yearly and its H1 FY27 numbers were not found.
