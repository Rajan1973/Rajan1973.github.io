The pressure point: the US tariff base

Op. margin change YoY, pts

The baseIn the September 2025 quarter US tariffs on Indian goods reached 50%. Exporters discounted to keep orders, and Welspun's profit nearly vanished.
The reliefA February 2026 India–US framework cut the tariff to about 18%. Welspun (+5.6 pts), Indo Count (+4.1) and Gokaldas (+2.9) recovered.
The other storySpinners KPR, Trident and Vardhman also widened margins QoQ, which a tariff base cannot explain. The driver was not verified.

What management said

From results releases and results coverage. No Q2 call transcripts were read.

Tariffs
  • Gokaldas: the year-ago quarter was hit by US tariffs; higher wages and oil-linked costs capped this quarter's gain.
Growth
  • Indo Count: utility bedding and Wamsutta contributed over ₹500 cr, 30% of income.
  • Welspun: aims for double-digit growth with low-teens margins.
Balance sheet
  • Welspun: net cash, from ₹775 cr net debt in March; cash cycle 44 days.
Operations
  • Welspun: Vapi operations disrupted by floods from 23 July; restoring them is the near-term priority.
  • Page: Q1 volume +5.7%, against a double-digit full-year target.

The call

Own now

Indo Count · KPR Mill

Indo Count is ahead of its own margin guide with new revenue lines. KPR has the best margin in the set and widened it QoQ, with a net-cash balance sheet; the stock is only +7% in a year.

Accumulate on evidence

Welspun · Vardhman · Page

Welspun must rebuild Vapi and lift margin into the low teens. Vardhman's spread must hold. Page must show double-digit volume.

Stay out

Raymond Lifestyle · Alok · Arvind

Other-income-dependent, loss-making and debt-funded respectively.

What would make us more positive

What would make us cut

Caveats

  • Sources. Financials are consolidated filed quarterlies from Tijori, with operating profit before other income. Commentary comes from BW Retail World, DSIJ, Angel One and Fibre2Fashion. No call transcripts were read; this is the weakest part of the report. AI digests were used only as leads.
  • Tijori against management. Welspun EBITDA ₹338 cr = Tijori OP. Gokaldas EBITDA ₹127 cr = OP + OI, at 11.2% of total income. Indo Count's 15.3% = (OP + OI) / total income; Tijori's margin before other income is 13.9%.
  • Tariff history. The 50% US tariff level in the base quarter and the February 2026 cut to about 18% are from press coverage; the effective date of the cut was not verified.
  • Data issues. Ambika's year-ago quarter in Tijori duplicates June 2025. Himatsingka's September quarter is not in Tijori. Alok's operating profit is off a near-zero base.
  • Exceptionals. Arvind's PBT includes a ₹28 cr gain; Alok's a ₹36 cr charge; Welspun's a ₹7 cr flood charge.
  • Dates. Results dates marked n/s were not verified.
