The call

Own now

GE Vernova T&D · CG Power

Both grew operating profit faster than revenue, with inflow up 52–78% and material share under 66%. GE Vernova T&D has the best margin in the set.

Accumulate on evidence

Hitachi Energy · TARIL · Voltamp · Bharat Bijlee

Hitachi's orders are a record but its margin needs to recover. TARIL's margin recovery needs one more quarter. Voltamp must pass costs on. Bharat Bijlee's growth comes at an 8% margin.

Stay out

Shilchar · Indo Tech · Salzer

Shilchar and Indo Tech lost 4–10 pts of margin. Salzer earns under 7% with a heavy interest bill.

What would make us more positive
  • Copper and CRGO prices easing in Q3.
  • Hitachi's reported margin back above 15%.
What would make us cut
  • GE Vernova T&D's or CG's margin falling as capacity adds dilute pricing.
  • A slowdown in utility tendering.
