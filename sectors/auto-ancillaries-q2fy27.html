Auto Ancillaries
Q2 FY27

First edition 15 Nov 2026 · Revised 19 Nov 2026

Revenue boomed and margins shrank. The median company grew revenue 30% on GST-cut demand, metal pass-through and acquisitions, while EBITDA grew only 23%. Margins fell at 13 of 15 companies. Copper, aluminium, steel and lead rose faster than OEM price resets, and the West Asia war added freight and fuel costs. Every management team says it will recover in one to two quarters. The same quarters also face last year's GST base.

The pass-through lag

Revenue growth minus EBITDA growth, percentage points (bigger = more margin lost)

Copper +54% YoYMSWIL's raw material cost reached ~70% of revenue, and its gross margin fell to ~30% from ~35% in FY24–25. Motherson's wiring harness and Craftsman's aluminium carry the same lag. Pass-through adds revenue without adding margin, so the percentage falls even when recovery is complete.
Not indexedFuel, freight, cutting tools and minimum wages are outside the metal-indexed contracts. Endurance said most OEMs did not compensate them in H1. Sona's freight rates nearly doubled QoQ. Schaeffler had recovered only ~50% of its inflation by quarter-end.
The promiseEndurance guides +1.0–1.1 pts of gross margin in Q3. Sundram guides +150 bps in H2, Amara Raja +1.5–2 pts, and Schaeffler a Q4 correction. TI's MD answered "100% yes" on an H2 EBITDA catch-up. Q3 is the test.

What management said

From the Q2 FY27 calls, presentations and releases. Grouped by theme.

Commodity pass-through
  • MSWIL: copper +54% YoY, passed through with a ~3-month lag (up to 6 for some customers). Copper added 12 pts to revenue growth.
  • Motherson: copper and aluminium pass-through lag of 3–6 months.
  • Schaeffler: raw-material inflation ~1.1% of sales, ~50% recovered. Historically it recovers 80–85%.
  • TI: ~15% volume growth; the rest of its 28% was HRC pass-through. Retrospective settlements take 1–2 quarters.
  • Uno Minda: casting revenue includes ₹260–270 cr of aluminium price.
Margin outlook
  • Endurance: Q3 gross margin +0.5 pt from OEM price increases (from 1 Oct) plus 0.5–0.6 pt from a cheaper metal basket.
  • Uno Minda: FY27 EBITDA margin 11% ±50 bps retained.
  • Sona: 23–25% band retained, but the CFO calls ~23% the "ballpark". The CEO: "all bets are off" if oil reaches $150.
  • Exide: 12–12.5% for the next two quarters at current lead and rupee.
  • Sundram: ~150 bps restored in H2 on a higher export share and cooler metals.
Demand and the H2 base
  • India PV +25% (MSWIL), 2W +18% and PV +24% (SIAM, per Endurance), CV +18.9% (Motherson).
  • Bosch: Q3 flat to modest growth against a high base; H2 "more modest". Tractors weak.
  • Exide: OEM growth moderates to low double digits in H2. Aftermarket is similar to H1.
  • MSWIL and Schaeffler: the GST 2.0 benefit enters the base from October.
  • Global LV production fell 2.3% in H1 (Motherson). EU growth was ~0.7% excluding Chinese OEMs (Endurance).
EV and new technology
  • Sona: BEV revenue +129%, 51% of auto sales. Traction and suspension motors run at ₹1,400 cr a year.
  • Exide: 6 GWh cell plant commissioned 23 Sep. LFP revenue from October; NMC to 2W OEMs from ~February. It carries a 5–10% cost gap against Chinese cells.
  • Amara Raja: new-energy loss ₹42 cr, up from ₹22 cr in Q1.
  • Minda Corp: EV is 10% of revenue and 35% of the order book. Endurance: EV sales +185%.
  • TI Clean Mobility: revenue ₹476 cr; truck EBITDA breakeven targeted for Q4 FY27.
Capex and balance sheets
  • Motherson: FY27 capex ₹6,000 cr, 60% non-auto. Net debt rose to ₹11,292 cr from ₹9,757 cr QoQ.
  • Amara Raja: FY27 capex ~₹2,200 cr (₹1,600 cr new energy), up from ₹1,700 cr guided in Q1.
  • Craftsman: gross debt ₹3,618 cr despite a ₹2,000 cr QIP. Target net debt/EBITDA 1.7–1.8x.
  • Endurance: net cash ₹436 cr and capex of ₹1,250 cr. Exide is debt-free, with ₹5,352 cr invested in EESL to date.
  • Uno Minda: net debt ₹2,415 cr (₹2,179 cr in March), with 16 projects worth ₹5,200–5,300 cr.
Exports and global
  • Sundram: standalone exports +57%. Class 8 truck backlog is high.
  • Schaeffler: exports +46% to group companies. Q3 is a seasonal spike, and Q4 will be lower.
  • Bharat Forge: Europe margin 3.1% and US EBITDA −₹6.7 cr. Defence order book ₹10,950 cr.
  • TI: exports stuck at ~15% of revenue against an aim of 20%+.
  • Uno Minda: exports from India ₹218 cr against ₹123 cr a year earlier.

Red flags

Ranked by how much they should change position sizing, not by how alarming they sound.

Act on these
Amara Raja
EBITDA fell 3.4% on revenue +18.5%. The margin fell for the second straight quarter, to 8.8% against a 13–14% target. Material cost rose to 66% of revenue from 60%. Capex guidance rose to ~₹2,200 cr while the new-energy loss widened QoQ.
Tube Investments
Standalone PBT was flat (₹253 cr against ₹250 cr) on 28% revenue growth. TI is now the sole funder of TI Clean Mobility, whose costs ran ~75% above trend, and its earlier breakeven targets have slipped. CG Power is carrying the consolidated profit.
MSWIL
Gross margin has fallen from ~35% to ~30% in two years. That is structural, not a timing lag. It gives no margin guidance, no absolute numbers on the call, and no balance-sheet data.
Monitor next quarter
Motherson
Net debt rose ₹1,535 cr QoQ after acquisitions. It plans ₹7,500 cr of consumer-electronics capex (from ₹2,600 cr). Its margin fell 20 bps to 8.8%.
Schaeffler
Working capital rose to ~19% of sales and Q3 free cash flow was ₹41 cr. Only half its inflation is recovered. The KRSV subsidiary runs at an EBITDA margin of −17.6%.
Bharat Forge
Consolidated EBITDA rose ~9% on revenue +21%. SBI Securities puts consolidated PAT 7% lower YoY. The overseas subsidiaries are near breakeven, and the stock trades at ~53x FY27E P/E (SBI Securities).
Craftsman
EBITDA rose 2% QoQ on revenue +21% QoQ. Gross debt is ₹3,618 cr after the QIP. Its margin was ~13.3% against ~15–16% last quarter.
Bosch
PAT +18.5% includes a ₹130 cr provision reversal. RBIC chassis margin was 15.6% against 18.5% in FY26. A 3:1 bonus and split are pending, so the per-share price will reset.
Note and move on
Exide
H1 includes a ~₹600 cr mark-to-market loss on the HDFC Life stake. The YoY margin gain is against a weak, GST-deferral base.
Endurance
Bajaj is ~35% of group income. PAT carries a ₹36 cr Stöferle gain against ₹31 cr of accelerated depreciation, which net to roughly zero.
ZF CV
The EBITDA margin fell 80 bps YoY to 13.9% while revenue grew 33%, so operating leverage did not show up.

The call

Own now

Uno Minda · Minda Corp · Sona BLW

Uno Minda hit its 11% margin guide while growing 38%, and new business is priced on today's metal base. Minda Corp is one of only two margin expanders, with EV at 35% of its order book. Sona's margin fell 2.3 pts on mix, but it made a record ₹446 cr of H1 free cash flow on a ₹251 bn order book. Sona is up 89% in a year, so size it for the valuation. Minda Corp fell 6% on its results day, which gives a better entry.

Accumulate on evidence

Motherson · Bosch · Exide · Endurance · Schaeffler · Sundram · Craftsman · ZF CV

Each one has a dated test in Q3. Endurance must deliver its +1 pt gross-margin guide. Sundram must restore 150 bps in H2. Schaeffler must correct margins and free cash flow in Q4. Exide needs its first NMC revenue around February. Motherson needs net debt to stop rising. Craftsman needs margin back above 15%. Bosch needs a Q3 against a high base. ZF CV needs the regulatory content (AEB, ESC) to offset the margin slide.

Stay out

Amara Raja · Tube Investments · MSWIL · Bharat Forge

Amara Raja's margin has fallen two quarters running, 4–5 pts below target, while capex rises. TI's standalone profit was flat, and its EV arm is a growing cash call funded by TI alone. MSWIL's gross margin is falling structurally. Bharat Forge's consolidated profit fell at a ~53x multiple. The market agrees on three: TI −26%, Amara Raja −22% and MSWIL −19% over a year. It disagrees on Bharat Forge, which is +25% over a year, though −15% over three months.

What would make us more positive
  • LME copper and aluminium down 10%+ from Q2 averages, with OEM price resets holding.
  • Q3 margins up QoQ at Endurance, Sundram and Schaeffler, as they guided.
  • Hormuz shipping normalising, which cuts freight and oil-derivative costs.
What would make us cut
  • Q3 volume growth below 10% as the GST base kicks in, which removes operating leverage.
  • Oil at $150, which Sona's CEO says breaks all margin assumptions.
  • Further capex or debt increases at Motherson, Amara Raja or Craftsman without a margin recovery.

Caveats

  • All 15 tracked companies have reported. ZF CV was last, on 13 Nov. Its call digest is a September investor deck, so its commentary is thin.
  • Many YoY margins are derived from reported EBITDA growth and revenue growth:
    • Bosch (~14.1% base)
    • Uno Minda (~11.4%)
    • Schaeffler (~20.2%)
    • MSWIL (~10.1%)
    • Endurance (~13.8%)
    The Q2 margins themselves are reported.
  • Bases differ.
    • Tube Investments is standalone PBT margin.
    • Exide and Schaeffler are standalone.
    • Schaeffler's quarter is Q3 CY26.
    • MSWIL disclosed growth rates only on its call; absolute figures were not checked against its filing.
  • Research route.
    • The first 11 companies come from BSE-filed call transcripts (Tapetide digests).
    • Amara Raja, Sundram, Craftsman, Minda Corp and ZF CV: reported figures are the filed results (BSE XBRL via DalalOS), checked against Tijori quarterlies; the two agree. Commentary comes from results coverage (Univest, Sahi, Autocar Pro).
  • Corporate actions. Kite prices are adjusted. ZF CV's 2026 5:1 bonus is already reflected. Bosch's 3:1 bonus and ₹2 split are pending; when they go ex, the CMP will reset by about 1/20.
  • Broker coverage is thin. Post-result targets were found for 3 of 15 names. The consensus section scores a narrative, not a poll.
