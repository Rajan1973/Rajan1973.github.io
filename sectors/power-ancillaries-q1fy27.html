08 / Red Flags & Accounting Forensics

CRGO Steel Supply Bottleneck

Import licensing or tariff changes on Electrical Steel (CRGO) can create production delays for transformer manufacturers.

Discom Payment Realization Friction

Delays in state discom billing approvals can stretch working capital days for smart meter suppliers.

AMISP Concession Debt Servicing

High capital commitment for smart meter installation mandates strict project execution to avoid interest drag.

10 / Sector Synthesis & Strategic Outlook

India's Power Ancillaries sector is in the midst of a multi-year super-cycle driven by renewable grid transmission expansion and national smart meter replacement (250M units target).

Market leaders with technology moats (TRIL for EHV Transformers, Genus for Smart Meters, Schneider for Substation Switchgear) offer high earnings visibility backed by record order books.
