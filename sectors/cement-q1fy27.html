What management said

From the Q1 FY27 calls and releases. Grouped by theme, because the companies said broadly the same things.

Demand — FY27
  • UltraTech reiterates double-digit volume growth.
  • Ambuja guides industry demand at ~5%.
  • Motilal Oswal pegs FY27 at 7–8%.
  • Nuvoco cites central capex +13% YoY to May 2026.
  • Drivers named: infrastructure, housing, rural.
Pricing
  • April hikes only partly stuck.
  • Net Q1 gain roughly ₹10–12/bag (Nuvama checks).
  • Managements expect prices broadly steady through monsoon.
  • Sagar reports prices flat March to July 2026.
Cost — the key guidance
  • UltraTech: +₹130–140/t sequentially in Q2. Costs near peak.
  • JK Cement: +₹150/t — fuel ₹75–100, diesel and logistics ~₹50.
  • Ambuja guides similar.
  • Easing expected only in H2 FY27.
West Asia impact
  • Cited by nearly every company.
  • Higher imported fuel, freight and shipping.
  • Ambuja flagged packaging inflation of 25–30%.
  • Supply-chain disruption, not just price.
Green power & premium mix
  • JK Lakshmi 49% green power — sector best.
  • UltraTech 45.6%, targeting 85% by FY30.
  • Ambuja 34%, targeting 60% by FY28.
  • India Cements 6% today, 80% by FY29.
  • Premium share of trade: ACC 44%, Ambuja 34%, Dalmia 25%.
Diversification
  • UltraTech into wires and cables: ₹1,800 cr, ₹888 cr committed, Q3 FY27 launch.
  • JK Cement paint: ₹125 cr Q1 revenue, EBITDA breakeven, ₹500–550 cr FY27 target.
  • RMC expansion at ACC (119 plants) and JK Cement (50 by year-end).
  • Both are margin-dilutive near term.

The call

Own now

UltraTech

The only company to grow EBITDA/t in a cost-shock quarter. Beat consensus, deleveraged to 0.87x, hit 200 MTPA a year early, and is turning India Cements around faster than guided. Green power at 45.6% is the structural hedge against exactly this fuel cycle.

Accumulate into Q2 weakness

Nuvoco · Shree · Ambuja

Management has pre-announced that Q2 is the peak-cost quarter. Expect a second leg down in the print and likely in the price. Nuvoco delivered record Q1 EBITDA and commissioned ahead of schedule; Shree and Ambuja carry net cash. Buy the cost peak, not before it.

Stay out

Sagar · Ramco · ACC standalone

Sagar is loss-making with leverage rising and its own FY27 guidance already unreachable. Ramco carries a structural Tamil Nadu land-tax drag of about ₹84 a tonne. ACC's earnings depend increasingly on intra-group supply arrangements with its own parent.

What would make us more positive
  • Price hikes of ₹15–20/bag sticking into Q3 FY27.
  • Petcoke sustained below $120/t.
  • Either one alone is not enough. Both together mean a broad H2 EBITDA/t rebound — buy the sector, not just the leaders.
What would make us cut
  • Any sign of market-share-led discounting among the top three. Consolidation is holding pricing discipline together; if that breaks, tier-2 has no cushion.
  • UltraTech specifically: net debt/EBITDA above 1.0x, or EBITDA/t below ₹1,150 in Q2.
  • Ambuja specifically: the ~80 MT FY27 target being quietly dropped.

Caveats

  • Reporting is incomplete. Star Cement reported 7 August. Smaller names may file up to the 14 August SEBI deadline. Granularity is thinner for the small caps.
  • Some EBITDA/t figures are derived. Company-reported for UltraTech, Ambuja, Dalmia, JK Cement, Ramco, Heidelberg, India Cements. Computed from EBITDA ÷ volume for ACC, Nuvoco, Shree, JK Lakshmi, Birla Corp, Sagar. Do not rank the derived ones against each other on small differences.
  • Year-on-year reads are distorted by M&A. UltraTech (India Cements, Kesoram), Ambuja (Penna, Sanghi, Orient), Dalmia (Jaiprakash), JK Lakshmi (Udaipur Cement). Sequential and like-for-like are more reliable this quarter.
  • Standalone and consolidated bases differ. Most visible at Shree Cement and JK Cement, where overseas and subsidiary operations now matter.
  • Beat/miss is confirmed only for UltraTech and JK Cement. For other names, consensus context comes from broker previews rather than confirmed post-result comparisons.
  • Regional absolute price levels are indicative. The reliable data is in change terms from brokerage channel checks. Absolute per-bag levels drawn partly from trade aggregators.
  • Forward statements are projections, not outcomes. Q2 cost guidance, H2 recovery, merger timing and FY27 capacity targets are all management or analyst views.
  • Ambuja capacity timing is ambiguous across sources. The near-term ~119 MTPA appears FY27-linked in the company release; the larger expansion has reportedly moved toward FY30. Treat with caution pending the investor presentation.
