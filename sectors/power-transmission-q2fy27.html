What management said

From the Q2 FY27 calls, releases, presentations and broker notes on them. Grouped by theme.

Capex and capitalisation
  • Power Grid raised FY27 capex to ₹370–400 bn and cut capitalisation to ₹250–300 bn. It expects ~₹400 bn of capex a year in FY28–29.
  • Adani Energy kept FY27 transmission capex at ~₹140 bn. It spent ₹79 bn in H1 and guides ₹180–190 bn for FY28.
  • Adani Energy capitalisation: ~₹80–90 bn in FY27, ~₹100 bn in FY28.
Orders and pipeline
  • Power Grid's works in hand are ~₹2.2 trn, about 89% TBCB. Q2 intake was ~₹525 bn, led by Barmer HVDC.
  • Adani Energy's under-construction book is ₹872 bn, +21.5% QoQ, after the Vizag and Satara wins.
  • Transrail: order book ₹14,218 cr, 93% T&D. It declared ₹1,747 cr of new orders in October.
  • Techno: order book ~₹10,100 cr. ₹2,511 cr of its ₹4,000 cr FY27 inflow target is secured.
Execution friction
  • Adani Energy: Halvad held up by right-of-way at ~50 tower locations. Navinal could slip to H1 FY28.
  • Adani Energy cut FY27 smart-meter installs to 8–9 mn from ~10 mn. It says the three causes are resolved.
  • Power Grid: right-of-way compensation rules are raising costs, and states are adopting them unevenly.
Guidance held
  • Techno: FY27 revenue ₹4,000 cr and standalone margin 13–14%. Management is "very comfortable".
  • Transrail: revenue growth 20–22% and ~11% EBITDA margin. H1 is "on track".
  • Power Grid's annual profit indication moved to "₹19,000+ cr".
New businesses
  • Techno: data-centre capex ~₹658 cr to date. Chennai has 5 MW live with ~500 kW leased. It gives no revenue timeline, citing fast-changing AI demand.
  • Techno: 85% of its 2.25 mn smart meters are deployed. It plans to monetise at least one asset in FY27.
  • Adani Energy's energy-trading platform EBIT was ₹8.0 bn, up from ₹0.1 bn. PL calls the spread a peak.
Balance sheets
  • Power Grid: debt-equity 1.42 (1.37); receivable days improved to 10.5 from 15.2.
  • Adani Energy: net debt/EBITDA modelled at 4.5x for FY27 by PL. 75–80% of its debt is fixed-rate.
  • Transrail's net debt fell to ₹143 cr from ₹267 cr, on ₹435 cr of H1 operating cash flow.

The call

Own now

Transrail · Techno Electric

The EPC side gets paid as the work is done, without waiting for capitalisation. Transrail grew 32% with a ₹14,218 cr book, net debt down to ₹143 cr and guidance on track. Techno grew 31% and reiterated ₹4,000 cr revenue. The 6% post-result fall prices in the data-centre doubt, so size Techno as an EPC company and treat the data centres as an option.

Accumulate on evidence

Power Grid · Skipper

Power Grid is a regulated compounder whose earnings lag its capex. Buy on proof that H2 capitalisation reaches the ₹250 bn floor. Skipper needs to disclose its order book against its own ₹10,000–11,000 cr target.

Stay out

Adani Energy · Jyoti Structures

At Adani Energy, a trading spread called a peak, smart-meter slippage, a right-of-way stall and 4.5x leverage outweigh the transmission book. Four brokers upgraded after a 30% fall, which is a valuation argument, not an execution one. Jyoti's disclosure is too thin to underwrite.

What would make us more positive
  • Power Grid capitalising more than ₹100 bn in Q3, putting the guide's upper half back in play.
  • Adani Energy closing Halvad and holding 8–9 mn smart-meter installs.
  • Techno disclosing leased data-centre MW above 2 MW, or monetising a smart-meter asset.
What would make us cut
  • TBCB bid intensity compressing tariffs as the pipeline shrinks from ₹1.19 trn to ₹672 bn.
  • Right-of-way cost pass-through being disallowed by state regulators.
  • EPC working capital stretching, with Transrail's net debt reversing the H1 decline.

Caveats

  • All six tracked companies have reported. Adani Energy was first, on 22 October. Techno Electric was last, on 12 November.
  • The core metric is missing. Neither asset owner discloses regulated RoE or regulated equity quarterly. PL's FY27E regulated equity for Power Grid (~₹1,013 bn) is a broker estimate.
  • Power Grid figures conflict across sources. H1 capex is ₹18,913 cr in the filing summary, ₹16,865 cr in PSU Watch and ~₹169 bn in PL. PAT growth is +8.4% consolidated (company) and +24% in PL's note, which may be standalone. H1 capitalisation is ₹9,300 cr (PSU Watch) and ₹93 bn (PL), which agree.
  • Adani Energy has two EBITDA lines. Reported ₹3,100 cr (30.8%) against adjusted ~₹3,400 cr including regulatory income. Its company PAT of ₹1,364 cr excludes a ~₹1,270 cr tax credit with an offsetting pass-through liability.
  • Skipper and Jyoti are thinly sourced. Skipper's margin is derived. For Jyoti only PAT was found.
  • The consensus section scores a narrative, not a poll. We found no single pre-season sector forecast to score against.
  • Primary filings were not reachable during the build. Figures come from company releases and presentations as summarised by exchange-filing services, plus broker notes.
