07 / Red Flags & Accounting Forensics

Inventory Carrying Risk on High Copper Prices

High LME copper prices increase raw material working capital requirements by 15-20%. Any sudden sharp drop in international copper prices creates inventory destocking write-down risks for retail-heavy players.

FMEG Segment Incubation Drag

Diversification into Fast-Moving Electrical Goods (fans, switches, lighting) by pure-play cable makers (RR Kabel, Polycab) continues to consume capital with lower margin profiles than core wire businesses.

Export Ocean Freight Volatility

Red Sea geopolitical disruptions and container shortages have increased shipping lead times and freight rates, impacting export margin realization for global suppliers like Apar and KEI.

09 / Sector Synthesis & Strategic Outlook

The Indian Cables & Wires Sector is benefiting from a structural multi-decade capex super-cycle across power distribution grid upgrades, renewable energy evacuation, high-speed rail, and real estate construction. Organized players continue to gain market share from unorganized regional producers as compliance, NABL accreditation, and brand trust take priority.

Market leaders with EHV technology capabilities and global export reach (Polycab, Apar, KEI) possess strong pricing power, permitting 100% pass-through of copper price inflation while generating industry-leading ROCEs (>25%).
