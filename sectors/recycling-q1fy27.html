09 / Sector Synthesis & Strategic Outlook

The Indian Recycling & Waste Management sector is undergoing a structural re-rating driven by strict regulatory enforcement of Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) norms and rising corporate ESG commitments. Companies possessing pan-India scrap collection networks and advanced value-addition capabilities (such as Gravita and Ganesha Ecosphere) are expanding EBITDA margins despite global commodity price volatility.
