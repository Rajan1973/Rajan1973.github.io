08 / Red Flags & Accounting Forensics

Quick Commerce Dark Store Rent & Logistics Inflation

Aggressive dark store expansion in prime urban micro-markets increases fixed lease costs and rider delivery incentives.

Unsecured Personal Loan Disbursal Friction

Tighter RBI risk-weight norms on consumer credit affect loan distribution take-rates for fintech platforms.

Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) Inflation

High digital ad spending across Google/Meta platforms mandates continuous monitoring of organic vs paid customer traffic ratios.

10 / Sector Synthesis & Strategic Outlook

Indian Platform Fintech & Consumer Internet companies have crossed the inflection point from cash burn to sustainable net profitability.

Zomato (Blinkit) and PB Fintech (Policybazaar) represent premier market monopolies with immense operating leverage and ad monetization potential over FY27-FY28.
