The call

Own now

Groww · CDSL

Groww's non-derivative lines grew fast enough to hold revenue flat through the auction shock, at a 64.5% margin. CDSL earns from market activity without option-premium risk and is −11% in a year.

Accumulate on evidence

BSE · Paytm · Pine Labs · Angel One

BSE needs September's recovery to show in Q3. Paytm's operating turnaround is real but its PBT still leans on other income. Pine Labs and Angel are improving from cost control and lower interest.

Stay out

PB Fintech · Jio Financial · KFin

PB Fintech's revenue model is under IRDAI review. Jio Financial's profit halved as it builds a lending book. KFin is buying growth at a margin cost.

What would make us more positive
  • IRDAI's final rules softer than the consultation paper.
  • BSE's Q3 options premium back above Q1 levels.
What would make us cut
  • Further SEBI curbs on F&O participation.
  • An adverse order on Paytm's FEMA notice.
