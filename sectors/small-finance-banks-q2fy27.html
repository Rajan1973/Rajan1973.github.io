What management said

From results releases and broker notes as reported in the press. No Q2 call transcript was read.

Funding
  • Jana: aims to improve its CASA ratio in H2 FY27.
  • Equitas: focus on granular deposits; deposits crossed ₹50,000 cr (+19%).
Mix
  • Jana: raise the secured share from 73.7% to 80%.
  • Ujjivan: secured loans 52% of the book, up 47% YoY.
  • Equitas: non-MFI advances +24%, led by gold loans (+193%).
Capital
  • Ujjivan: ₹409 cr preferential issue to IFC and three life insurers.
  • AU: final universal-bank application submitted.
Leadership
  • Equitas: CEO resigned over a perceived conflict of interest; a successor is expected within three months.
  • Ujjivan: Acting MD and CEO since September.

The call

Own now

Ujjivan

The only bank where PPOP grew as fast as the provision benefit (+50%). Highest NIM, falling NPAs, fresh capital priced above book. The interim CEO is the one gap.

Accumulate on evidence

AU · Jana · ESAF

AU needs its NIM to stop falling. Jana needs CASA above 20%. ESAF needs a source for its GNPA collapse before the +72% run is trusted.

Stay out

Equitas · Utkarsh

Equitas has margin compression and a leadership vacuum at once. Utkarsh is still loss-making.

What would make us more positive
  • Q3 NIM stable QoQ at AU and Equitas.
  • A named external CEO at Equitas.
What would make us cut
  • Provisions rising QoQ at two or more banks, as already at Utkarsh.
  • Further deposit rate hikes, or new state microfinance laws.

Caveats

  • Sources. Financials are filed quarterlies from Tijori. NII = interest earned − interest expended; PPOP and provisions as filed. GNPA, credit cost, targets and broker views come from press coverage of results: PSU Connect, Kotak Neo, IndiaIPO, Angel One, HDFC Sky (PTI), Business Today and TradingView. AI digests were used only as leads.
  • Tijori against management. Ujjivan NII ₹1,248 cr (Tijori) vs ₹1,244 cr (company); a rounding-level gap. AU PAT ₹858 cr (Tijori) vs ₹860 cr (Nuvama). ESAF NII ₹644 cr matches ₹643 cr.
  • Suryoday. Tijori files it in a corporate format. NII is derived; NIM, CASA and cost-to-income are not available. Provisions of ₹41 cr and GNPA come from PTI.

  • ESAF GNPA. The fall from 5.40% to 2.84% QoQ is unexplained in what we read. The December 2025 ARC sale explains only the earlier fall.
  • Dates. Jana's results date is not verified. Price at report is not shown for broker targets.
