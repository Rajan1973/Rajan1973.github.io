The cost shockTimken said carbides are up ~200%, with steel, gases and labour also higher. Its raw-material ratio rose 130 bps QoQ, about half from mix and half from inflation. SKF India blamed steel and supplier value-addition costs. Menon faced tin +72% and copper +50%. Schaeffler's inflation equals ~1.1% of sales.
The recovery gapSchaeffler has recovered ~50% and historically recovers 80–85%, over 4–6 months. SKF India promises the recovery "largely" in the December quarter, and above 15% (including other income) within FY27. Timken warned that auto customers may delay pass-through. Industrial customers re-price faster than auto OEMs.
The offsetExports carry a higher margin and are growing fastest: Timken ₹263 cr (+60% YoY, +30% QoQ) and Schaeffler +46% to group companies. Both warned that the October–December quarter softens seasonally, so Q3 loses some of this cushion.

What management said

From the Q2 calls and presentations. SKF India (Industrial) did not hold a call.

Input costs
  • Timken: carbides ~+200%; steel, gases and labour higher. "The industry must pass costs on."
  • SKF India: steel inflation and supplier value-addition costs. Customer price talks are not concluded.
  • Menon: tin +72%, copper +50%, steel +12%. Gross margin down over 7 pts YoY; price revisions are being passed through.
  • Schaeffler: inflation ~1.1% of sales, ~50% recovered. Price recoveries take 4–6 months and are mostly retrospective.
Demand
  • Schaeffler: automotive demand is "strong for at least some quarters of 2027". The GST 2.0 base kicks in from October.
  • Timken: the Q3 setup is "fairly positive". Rail is slightly delayed, with the tender committee due in 2–3 weeks.
  • NRB: growth guide moved to 19%+ from 15%. Industrial +48%, CV +47%.
  • Menon: growth above 30% guided for FY27 and FY28; H1 +47%.
  • SKF Industrial: positioned to outgrow wind, heavy industries, aftermarket and exports (no numbers).
Exports
  • Timken: exports ₹263 cr, +60% YoY, 27% of revenue. October–December exports usually soften.
  • Schaeffler: exports +46%, now 17% of revenue. Q3 is a seasonal spike; the >₹500 cr run rate "is not a baseline".
  • SKF India: exports are 10% of sales.
  • Menon: exports 34–35% of sales, short of the ~37% guided on the Q1 call.
Capacity
  • Timken Bharuch: SRB lines reached 70%, the August–September target set on the Q1 call. CRB lines are at 58–60%, with their ramp guided for Q2-end and Q3. Bharuch revenue rose to ~₹75 cr from ~₹50 cr in Q1.
  • Timken: ₹120 cr rail expansion with Jamshedpur trials in November–December. ₹70 cr GGB lines installed but not capitalised.
  • SKF India: ₹500 cr+ expansion plan, ~₹200 cr this fiscal; new capacity live around July 2027.
  • SKF Industrial: ₹900–950 cr Pune plant, production from 2028 (Q1 call). A new Ahmedabad CRB line adds 0.22 mn units.
EV and new products
  • SKF India: hybrid-transmission driveline win (ceramic bearings, conductive bushes), contributing from FY28.
  • Schaeffler: unplanned e-mobility inventory. The Mobility margin was hurt by e-mobility development costs.
  • NRB: Unitec JV commissions by April 2027, targeting ~₹130 cr of revenue at scale.
Margin outlook
  • Schaeffler: a Q4 margin correction as recoveries land.
  • SKF India: above 15% (including other income) within FY27. Its Q1 call had called ~17% "normalised" for two years.
  • NRB: 18–20% over a year, "not quarter by quarter" (Q1 call).
  • Timken: no numeric guidance. It declined to quantify the industrial vs auto margin gap.

SKF India (auto)
Operating margin fell 5.3 pts QoQ to 9.6% on revenue +1.4%. Operating profit fell 35% and PBT 17% QoQ. Without ₹29 cr of other income, mostly from SKF Industrial, the 14.5% headline would be 9.6%. Aftermarket is down ~7%. Capex is stepping up to ₹500 cr+.
SKF India (Industrial)
Operating profit −6.5% YoY on revenue +28%. PBT +33% came from other income of ₹45 cr (₹17 cr a year ago). Operating cash flow was −₹6 cr against +₹218 cr a year ago. No earnings call. Dec-25 quarter had a ₹180 cr exceptional charge.
Monitor next quarter

The call

Own now

Timken India

Timken grew operating profit 28.7% on revenue +25% and held its margin at 18.7% through the cost shock. Exports are now 27% of revenue. The stock is flat over a year and −12% over six months, and it has kept its Q1-call promises: SRB lines hit 70% on schedule and Bharuch revenue rose by half. Two triggers are near-term: rail tenders, and the CRB lines ramping in Q3.

Accumulate on evidence

Schaeffler · NRB Bearings · Menon Bearings

Schaeffler needs to show its Q4 margin correction and a free-cash-flow rebound. NRB beat on growth, but its margin is at the floor of its band and depreciation will keep PAT behind; the stock is up 48% in a year. Menon has the best fundamentals in the sector: operating margin +4.3 pts YoY, PAT +117%, and it is ahead of its own guide. But the stock is +214% in a year and +33% this month, at ~35x annualised H1 earnings (derived). Own the business on a pullback, not at this price.

Stay out

SKF India (automotive) · SKF India (Industrial)

SKF India is the most auto-OEM-exposed business, with the slowest price recovery and the sharpest QoQ margin fall. It missed both scorable Q1 promises, and capex is stepping up. Its hybrid win pays from FY28. The stock is −11% over six months. SKF Industrial moves here from "accumulate". Its 34% PAT growth came from other income while operating profit fell, and it held no call. Revisit when the operating margin is back above 12%.

What would make us more positive
  • Steel, carbide and metal prices rolling over, with Q3 operating margins up QoQ.
  • Indian Railways tender awards landing in Q3 for Timken and SKF Industrial.
  • Timken CRB lines at Bharuch reaching SRB-level utilisation (~70%) by December.
What would make us cut
  • Q3 operating margins down again QoQ, which would mean the pass-through is failing, not lagging.
  • Exports falling more than seasonally on European demand or tariffs.
  • Another negative-cash quarter at SKF Industrial or Schaeffler.

  • The published Q1 Bearings report is not a usable baseline.

    • It treated SKF India as one entity after the 2025 demerger.
    • It omitted SKF Industrial from the cards.
