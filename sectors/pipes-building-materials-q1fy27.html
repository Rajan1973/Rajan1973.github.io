1. The Thesis: Operating Leverage After Destocking

Plastic pipe manufacturers demonstrated sharp volume recovery in Q1 FY27 (+18.5% aggregate volume growth), as plumbing and real estate demand surged while anti-dumping duties on PVC resin imports from China and Taiwan provided raw material pricing certainty. High-value CPVC plumbing, bath fittings, and adhesives diversification continue to expand EBITDA spreads for organized leaders.
