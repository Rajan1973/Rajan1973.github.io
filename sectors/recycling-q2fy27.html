The call

Own now

Ganesha Ecosphere · Tinna Rubber

Both recycle domestic feedstock, avoided the import-scrap squeeze, and widened margins. Ganesha's margin has held at 12–14% for three quarters; Tinna grew operating profit 37%.

Accumulate on evidence

Gravita · MSTC

Gravita's spreads held, but volume must recover from +3%. MSTC's EPR-platform role is real but the stock has already run 46% this month.

Stay out

Jain Resource · Pondy Oxides · Antony Waste

Jain and Pondy lost spread to scrap scarcity. Antony reported a loss.

What would make us more positive
  • Gravita's Q3 volume growth back in double digits.
  • EPR credit trading going live on MSTC.
What would make us cut
  • Restrictions on scrap imports, or the shortage spreading to domestic PET.
  • Lead prices falling sharply, which would hit spreads.
