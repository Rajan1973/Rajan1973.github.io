What management said

From Q2 call transcripts (PI, Vinati, Navin) and results releases (SRF, Aarti, Clean Science).

Input costs
  • Vinati: raw materials passed on; energy and logistics not. Logistics rates cooling in Q3.
  • Navin: sulphur, gas and fluorspar up; offset by pricing and forex.
  • Aarti: pass-through protected absolute EBITDA but suppressed margins.
Demand
  • PI: genericisation arriving faster than expected; agchem exports −18%.
  • Vinati: ATBS destocking; Q3 volumes looking strong.
  • SRF: specialty chemicals hit by Chinese overcapacity.
Guidance
  • Vinati: FY27 margin 24–25% (was 25–30%); FY28 25–26%.
  • PI: FY27 growth "flattish" (was low single digit).
  • Navin: FY27 margin near H1's 34%; long-term 30% unchanged.
Capex
  • Navin: ₹700 cr in FY27, the low end of its ₹700–1,000 cr range.
  • Vinati: ₹500 cr approved, a further ₹500 cr under study.
  • SRF: ₹600 cr battery-grade aluminium foil plant.

The call

Own now

Navin Fluorine · Fine Organic

The only two whose margins held QoQ. Navin delivered above its own guide with CDMO visibility; Fine is net cash with margin up 2.8 pts YoY.

Accumulate on evidence

SRF · Aarti · Gujarat Fluoro

Each grew strongly but at a lower margin. Buy when Q3 gross margin stops falling as input costs ease.

Stay out

PI Industries · Vinati · Atul

PI and Vinati cut guidance. Atul took the steepest gross-margin hit.

What would make us more positive
  • Q3 gross margin recovering QoQ as crude and logistics costs ease.
  • Vinati's ATBS volume recovery showing up in Q3.
What would make us cut
  • Phenol spreads normalising, which would hit Deepak.
  • A further PI guide cut, or Chinese supply restraint ending.

Caveats

  • Sources. Financials are consolidated filed quarterlies from Tijori, with operating profit before other income. Commentary comes from the PI and Vinati transcripts (Tijori), Navin's transcript (NSE), and results coverage in Indian Chemical News, Free Press Journal, Angel One, ThePrint and Open (Systematix). AI digests were used only as leads.
  • Tijori against management. Navin EBITDA ₹407 cr = Tijori OP. Vinati consolidated EBITDA ₹138 cr = OP + OI. Aarti EBITDA ₹418 cr = OP + OI, but its revenue of ₹3,887 cr exceeds Tijori's ₹3,669 cr; unreconciled, both shown. PI's 49% gross margin is the company's own figure; the Tijori-derived figure is 53.9%.
  • Core metric. Volume was not available on a comparable basis. The scissor uses revenue and operating-profit growth instead. This is the weakest part of the report.
  • Exclusions. Alkyl Amines: Tijori's quarterly data stops in March 2020. Sudarshan includes Heubach from March 2025, so its YoY is not like-for-like before that.
  • Dates. Results dates marked n/s were not verified.
