The call

Own now

NTPC · NHPC · JSW Energy

NTPC grew generation 13% at a fleet PLF well above peers and is −19% over six months. NHPC's new capacity lifted operating profit 42% even after interest doubled. JSW grew operating profit faster than interest.

Accumulate on evidence

SJVN · Adani Green · NTPC Green · Adani Power

SJVN's step-change needs a second quarter. The renewables need interest growth to slow. Adani Power's underlying growth is 13%, not the headline.

Stay out

NLC India · JP Power · CESC · Torrent

NLC and JP Power rest on tax credits or carry large exceptional charges. CESC has no growth. Torrent's interest doubled and other income propped up PBT.

What would make us more positive
  • NLC's PBT recovering without tax credits.
  • Interest growth at the renewables falling below EBITDA growth.
What would make us cut
  • A weak monsoon-to-winter demand turn in Q3 generation data.
  • Discom receivables stretching at the PSUs.

Caveats

  • Sources. All financials are consolidated filed quarterlies from Tijori, with operating profit before other income. Commentary comes from the NTPC and Adani Power releases and press reports of NTPC's operational update.
  • Tijori against management.
    • Adani Power: reported EBITDA ₹8,743 cr = Tijori operating profit ₹8,231 cr + other income ₹512 cr. It reconciles. The underlying figure removes ₹2,422 cr of prior-period revenue; this is our adjustment.
