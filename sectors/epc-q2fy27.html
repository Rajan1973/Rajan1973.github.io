The call

Own now

NCC · Engineers India

NCC grew revenue and operating profit by a third with the margin intact, and is −27% in a year. Engineers India added 8 pts of margin with no debt; it is the one stock the market already rewards (+56%).

Accumulate on evidence

L&T · IRB · Kalpataru · HG Infra

L&T's orders are unmatched, but the margin must stop slipping. IRB and Kalpataru are deleveraging. HG Infra's numbers are good, but the −54% price fall needs an explanation first.

Stay out

KEC · Afcons · PNC · Ashoka

KEC missed both Q1 commitments. Afcons' operating profit collapsed. PNC is barred from its main client for three years. Ashoka is loss-making after an exceptional charge.

What would make us more positive
  • KEC's Q3 margin back above 7% as the Middle East eases.
  • L&T's core margin stabilising above 9% on management's basis.
  • Afcons' revenue growing again.
What would make us cut
  • NCC's interest cost rising faster than operating profit.
  • Further NHAI debarments across the road contractors.

Caveats

  • Sources. All financials are consolidated filed quarterlies from Tijori, with operating profit before other income. Commentary comes from Kotak Neo, Afcons' BSE press release and Business Today, because Tijori had no Q2 transcripts for this set by 15 November.
  • Tijori against management.
    • L&T: management EBITDA ₹6,845 cr (8.7%) against Tijori operating profit ₹8,814 cr (11.2%). The ~₹1,970 cr gap matches L&T Finance's interest cost, which Tijori places below operating profit. Both bases show a 1.3-pt fall YoY. Revenue and net profit match exactly.
    • KEC: EBITDA ₹271 cr and 5.0% match exactly.
    • Afcons: management's H1 EBITDA is ₹578 cr (10.3%). Tijori's H1 operating profit is ₹343 cr before other income and ₹670 cr after it. We could not reconcile this, so both are shown.
  • Comparability. IRB is a toll-road owner. Ashoka deconsolidated road assets in December 2025. PNC's PAT fall is a base effect from a ₹163 cr exceptional gain last year.
  • Prices. Kite returns are corporate-action adjusted. An NSE corporate-action cross-check for KEC and HG Infra failed to load; the price falls are consistent with their results and news.
  • Walk the talk and analyst coverage are thin. Results dates marked n/s were not verified.
