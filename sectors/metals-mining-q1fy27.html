08 / Red Flags & Accounting Forensics

Global Steel Dumping Impact

Chinese domestic property slowdown pushing cheap HRC exports into SEA and India markets, requiring watchful monitoring of domestic realization spreads.

Parent Holding Debt & Dividend Payout Pressure

Vedanta Resources debt maturity obligations require continued high dividend cash upstreaming from operating subsidiaries like Hindustan Zinc.

UK & European Carbon Border Adjustments (CBAM)

Implementation of EU CBAM regulations will mandate carbon audits and green steel transition capex for Indian metal exporters.

10 / Sector Synthesis & Strategic Outlook

Indian Metals & Mining sector is insulated from global commodity weakness by strong domestic infrastructure capex, automotive production growth, and government anti-dumping policy support.

Integrated producers with 100% captive iron ore/coal assets (Tata Steel, JSPB, NMDC) and low-cost aluminium champions (Hindalco) offer superior risk-reward profiles.
