09 / Sector Synthesis & Strategic Outlook

The Speciality Chemicals sector is exhibiting signs of an operational bottom after a prolonged 18-month destocking cycle in global agrochemical channels. Leaders with proprietary fluorination technology (GFL, SRF) and scale-backed import substitution moats (Deepak Nitrite) are outperforming commodity intermediate producers. Profitability is turning favorable as raw material prices stabilize and volume recovery gains traction into H2 FY27.
