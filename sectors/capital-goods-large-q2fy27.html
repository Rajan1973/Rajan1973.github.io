The pressure point: commodity and freight costs

Op. margin change YoY, pts

The cost shockCummins: "Commodity and freight inflation, coupled with product mix, impacted margins." Siemens: "volatility in commodity prices and foreign exchange". AIA's selling and distribution costs rose 53%.
Who absorbed itEngine makers (Cummins −6.6 pts, Kirloskar −4.8) and AIA (−2.9). Siemens grew operating profit only 3% on revenue +17%; Mobility profit fell.
Who did notElgi (+1.9 pts) and Grindwell (+0.7) held or widened margins on volume. BHEL (+3.3) is recovering from a low base and benefits from lower interest costs.

The call

Own now

Elgi Equipments · Grindwell Norton

Elgi grew operating profit 32% on revenue +17%, the best operating leverage in the set. Grindwell held its margin and grew PAT 24%. Neither has run as hard as BHEL or ABB: +17% and +21% in a year.

Accumulate on evidence

Siemens · ABB India

Siemens' orders (+38%) and backlog (₹47,034 cr) are the best demand signal here, but the margin must stop falling. ABB's margin recovered QoQ but is still below last year. Up 42% in a year, it needs Q4 order confirmation.

Stay out

Cummins · BHEL · AIA · Kirloskar · Thermax

Cummins lost 6.6 pts of margin with no verified fix. BHEL's profit surge is real, but Q2 inflow stalled and the stock is +55%. AIA and Kirloskar are growing without profit. Thermax runs a 7% margin.

What would make us more positive
  • Cummins' Q3 margin back above 18% as price increases land.
  • BHEL's H2 inflow back above ₹20,000 cr.
  • Commodity and freight costs rolling over.
What would make us cut
  • Siemens' order growth fading below revenue growth.
  • Another quarter of margin decline at Siemens or ABB.

Caveats

  • Sources. All financials are consolidated filed quarterlies from Tijori, with operating profit before other income. Commentary comes from news reports of results statements (IANS/The Hans India, Kotak Neo, Free Press Journal, T&D India), because Tijori had no Q2 transcripts for this set by 15 November.
  • Tijori against management.
    • Cummins' consolidated PAT of ₹507 cr matches the reports; the ₹467 cr figure in some headlines is standalone.
    • Siemens' 9.5% margin is after depreciation: ₹626 cr operating profit less ₹89 cr of depreciation ≈ ₹545 cr reported.
    • BHEL's PBT is ₹1,089 cr in the filing against ₹1,071 cr in Tijori, an ~₹18 cr gap we could not trace.
  • Fiscal years. Siemens now reports April–March (this is Q2 FY27). ABB reports a calendar year (Q3 CY26). Siemens Energy India (September year-end) had not reported.
  • Universe. Timken and SKF India are in the sector list but are covered in the Bearings report, so they are excluded here. Siemens Energy India is added after the 2025 demerger.
  • Walk the talk and analyst coverage are thin. These are the weakest sections. Results dates marked n/s were not verified.
