The pressure point: the squeeze moved to the hardware makers

Op. margin change YoY, pts (HBL −18.1 off-scale)

The cost shockThe Middle East war pushed up input costs across the chain. Cells and modules (Premier), lead, sulphuric acid and poly (Amara Raja), and copper, aluminium and freight (EPC) all rose. Sterling & Wilson says battery prices rose in 2026 after years of decline.
Who absorbed itThe manufacturers. Waaree Energies lost 5.4 pts and Premier 2.6 pts. Amara Raja missed its own promise to recover cost inflation by Q2. Hitachi's reported margin fell 2.8 pts, mostly an FX timing loss.
Who did notOwners with contracted storage. ACME's BESS earns 78.3% and it has ₹3,100 cr of short-term revenue locked in for FY27–28. Exide was the exception among makers: price increases took its margin up 1.6 pts.

What management said

From Q2 call transcripts where Tijori had them by 15 November (JSW, Waaree Energies, Premier, Exide, ACME, Sterling & Wilson, Waaree Renewable). Hitachi Energy, Amara Raja and Tata Power are from their Q2 presentations and Q1 calls.

Storage demand
  • Sterling & Wilson: storage becomes mandatory at new solar and wind projects from July 2027. "Most large solar tenders" will include it.
  • Waaree Renewable: CEA estimates ~208 GWh of storage needed by 2030. About 144 GWh of projects over 1 MWh are in the pipeline; 7.5 GWh is commissioned.
  • Green Energy Corridor Phase III (₹1.86 lakh cr) includes 50 GWh of BESS by FY32–33 (cited by Hitachi and Waaree Renewable).
Who is building what
  • Exide: 6 GWh lithium cell plant commissioned.
  • Waaree Energies: 5 GWh pack and container plant in Q3 FY27; 3.5 GWh cells this year.
  • Amara Raja: 10 GWh BESS plant; board approved a 5 GWh LFP cell plant and 9 GWh of pack capacity.
  • Premier: BESS unit in "Q2 2027", aiming for 40% domestic content.
  • JSW Energy: ~1 GWh of storage to commission in FY27.
Storage margins
  • ACME: BESS EBITDA margin 78.3% as an asset owner.
  • Amara Raja: BESS manufacturing margin guided at 5–8%, close to the pack business.
  • Hitachi: BESS margins to "gradually reach the desired levels" as it localises.
Costs and pricing
  • Premier: Middle East war inflation raised raw-material prices and delayed deliveries.
  • Sterling & Wilson: battery prices rose in 2026; it is selective on BESS bids.
  • Amara Raja (Q1): ~3% price hikes in June, 2–3% more in August. Not enough: margin fell again.
Guidance
  • Waaree Energies: FY27 operating EBITDA ₹7,000–7,700 cr reaffirmed; 55–60% expected in H2.
  • JSW Energy: 3 GW in FY27, "confident"; 30 GW by 2030.
  • Genus: FY27 revenue ₹6,000–6,500 cr maintained (trade press).
  • Waaree Renewable: FY27 margin "more than 15%".

Red flags

Ranked by how much they should change position sizing.

Act on these
Waaree Energies
Operating profit −5% YoY on revenue +24%, and the margin fell 5.4 pts. H1 is 36–40% of a reaffirmed full-year EBITDA guide. Hitting the low end needs a much stronger H2, while new cell lines are still stabilising.
Amara Raja
It missed the Q1-call promise to recover costs by Q2/Q3, and exports fell 18%. Current borrowings doubled to ₹547 cr as lithium capex runs. Its own BESS margin guide is 5–8%.
Waaree Renewable
Growth is acquired (APSPL), funded ~75% with debt. Interest rose 3.8x QoQ and PAT fell 30%. Solar EPC execution halved on land delays at the developer's end.
Monitor next quarter
Genus Power
The ₹6,000–6,500 cr FY27 guide needs ₹3,500–4,000 cr in H2, against H1 of ₹2,498 cr. Q2 revenue fell 17% QoQ.
Tata Power
Net profit +48.6% includes a ₹119 cr exceptional gain. Before it, PAT grew 4.4% and the margin fell 2.7 pts.
HBL Engineering
It missed a major Kavach tender, and the stock fell ~13%. Revenue −29% and margin −18 pts YoY on a Kavach-spiked base.
KPI Green · ACME Solar
Interest and depreciation are front-loaded as assets are capitalised. KPI's interest doubled; ACME's rose 30%.
Note and move on
Hitachi Energy
A ₹56 cr FX and commodity timing loss sits in operating costs. It is a reporting artefact, not a demand problem.
Disclosure
Only ACME gives a storage margin. Everyone else's "BESS story" is capacity plans and order mentions.

The call

Own now

Exide Industries · ACME Solar

Exide has a commissioned 6 GWh cell plant, a margin that rose while its peers' fell, and a debt-free core. The stock is −22% over three months. ACME is the only listed name earning storage revenue at scale: 5.2 GWh, a 78% BESS margin and ₹3,100 cr of contracted short-term revenue. It is +65% in a year, so size it for execution risk, not upside.

Accumulate on evidence

Hitachi Energy · JSW Energy · Premier Energies

Hitachi's backlog is a record. Its first BESS order is a test, and Q3 must show the FX noise reversing. JSW's capacity delivery is on time, but leverage (~4.9x) caps upside until storage scales. Premier has the best margins among manufacturers and its 7 GW cell line is live. Wait for commercial production and for the margin to stop falling.

Stay out

Waaree Energies · Amara Raja · Waaree Renewable · HBL

Waaree Energies needs a heroic H2 to hit its guide. Amara Raja missed its recovery promise and guides BESS at 5–8%. Waaree Renewable's growth is acquired with debt. HBL's Kavach pipeline, not batteries, drives it. Sterling & Wilson, KPI Green, Servotech and High Energy are too small or too unclear to carry a storage thesis.

What would make us more positive
  • A second listed company disclosing a storage segment margin.
  • Waaree Energies posting a Q3 operating margin above 20%.
  • Exide naming cell customers and utilisation.
What would make us cut
  • Battery and cell prices rising further, squeezing manufacturer margins again.
  • The July 2027 storage mandate being diluted or deferred.
  • ACME's short-term BESS tariffs resetting lower on new supply.
