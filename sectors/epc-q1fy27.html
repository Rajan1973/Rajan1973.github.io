09 / Sector Synthesis & Strategic Outlook

The EPC infrastructure sector experienced a bifurcated Q1 FY27. Players with significant international exposure—particularly in Middle East power transmission, substation, and hydrocarbon projects (L&T, Kalpataru)—navigated domestic government tendering slowdowns with ease. Conversely, pure-play domestic road EPC contractors suffered from election-related project sanctioning delays and monsoon disruptions, resulting in margin compression and revenue declines.
