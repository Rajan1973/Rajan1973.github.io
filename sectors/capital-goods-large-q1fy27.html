07 / Red Flags & Accounting Forensics

Working Capital Lockup in Large EPC Contracts

State power discom and thermal power EPC projects carry long billing milestone approval timelines, stretching trade receivables for BHEL and Thermax up to 180 days.

Parent Royalty & Technology Licensing Fees

MNC subsidiaries (Siemens, ABB, Cummins) pay 2-3% of turnover in technology royalty fees to global parents, capping net profit margin potential.

Commodity Price Exposure (Specialty Alloys)

Fluctuations in nickel, copper, and special grade boiler steel affect unhedged fixed-price long-cycle contracts if execution delays occur.

09 / Sector Synthesis & Strategic Outlook

The Large Capital Goods Sector in India is undergoing a powerful multi-year structural expansion driven by a twin engine: Government-led energy grid & railway capex alongside private industrial capacity additions (metals, cement, data centers, electronics).

With sector order books standing at an all-time high of ₹1.85 Lakh Cr (~3.4x revenue coverage), companies with high technology intensity (ABB, Siemens, Cummins) are commanding premium pricing power and expanding margins above historical averages.
