What management said

From the Q1 FY27 releases, calls and transcripts reviewed. Grouped by theme.

Gold-loan yield & bank competition
  • Muthoot: last year's yield was a "windfall," unlikely to recur; FY27 profit growth guided "limited to single digits."
  • Muthoot flags the NBFC gold-loan book (~₹4 lakh cr) is still far smaller than banks' comparable book — competitive pressure from both sides.
  • Manappuram and IIFL both report gold yield improving QoQ, taking share while Muthoot gives up pricing.
  • Manappuram guides 25–30% gold-loan AUM growth for FY27 plus 500 new branches.
Vehicle finance & rural cycle
  • L&T Finance: "disciplined execution amidst an evolving macro environment... geopolitical uncertainties, inflationary pressures and elevated borrowing costs."
  • L&T Finance's Lakshya 2031 plan targets book growth >20%/yr, credit cost <2%, RoA 3–3.2%, RoE 16–18%.
  • Shriram Finance guides FY27 AUM growth of 15–18% and NIM of 8.5–9.0%, aided by lower cost of funds.
  • Sundaram Finance flags interest costs rising faster than revenue as the quarter's margin drag.
Capital & governance events
  • Shriram Finance: MUFG equity infusion lifts CRAR from 20.40% to 34.17% — a funding event, not an underwriting one.
  • Manappuram: Bain Capital invests ₹4,385cr for an 18% stake (RBI-cleared to 41.7% via open offer); founder V P Nandakumar moves to non-executive chairman.
  • Manappuram's incoming CEO Ashish Singh (ex-IDFC FIRST) takes charge from 1 January 2027.
  • IIFL Finance: management notes "any equity raise would impact ROE" against its 16–20% target band.
Subsidiary and segment stress
  • Cholamandalam's insurance arm (Chola MS General): combined ratio worsened to 120.4% from 114.8%; PAT fell 19.7% to ₹86cr.
  • Manappuram's Asirvad Microfinance returned to profit (₹21cr) but AUM fell 5.8% QoQ.
  • Manappuram's Vehicle & Equipment Finance unit has paused new disbursements to focus on collections and asset-quality recovery.
  • L&T Finance's Personal Loans book grew 80% YoY (126% on disbursements) off a small base — a segment to watch, not yet a flag.
Diversification promises vs. delivery
  • CSB Bank: gold loans rose to 54% of advances from 47% a year ago, even as management restates a plan to bring the mix to ~30% by FY2030.
  • CSB Bank guides deposit growth of 25%+ and an ROE goal of ~15%, up from FY26's 14.26%.
  • Poonawalla Fincorp: management cites "no concerning trends" in early delinquencies across segments.
  • IIFL Finance guides ~25% AUM growth for FY27 with credit cost of 1.5–1.7%.

The call

Own now

Shriram Finance · L&T Finance

Shriram delivered against every metric in its own FY27 guidance and raised capital from MUFG on top. L&T Finance is running ahead of the >20% book-growth leg of its newly launched Lakshya 2031 plan. Both are the cleanest prints in the set with a normal prior-year base.

Wait for clarity

Cholamandalam · Manappuram · IIFL · Sundaram Finance · Poonawalla Fincorp

Each has a genuinely strong core business obscured by something else: Cholamandalam's insurance losses, Manappuram's leadership transition, IIFL's inconsistent PAT reporting and low base, Sundaram's thin disclosure and margin drag, Poonawalla's low-base growth rate. Own the businesses; wait for the noise to clear over the next one or two quarters.

Stay out for now

Muthoot Finance · CSB Bank · Mahindra Finance

Muthoot is the largest gold lender losing the most pricing power, with management itself cautious on FY27 profit. CSB Bank is moving further from its own diversification target and the stock has already voted with a 6.7% post-result drop. Mahindra Finance combines the highest disclosed leverage in the set with an asset-quality disclosure gap this quarter.

What would make us more positive
  • Muthoot's gold-loan yield stabilising or rising QoQ next quarter rather than continuing to erode.
  • Manappuram's incoming CEO articulating a clear FY27–29 roadmap without disrupting the gold-loan growth engine.
  • CSB Bank's gold-loan mix actually turning down toward its own stated FY2030 path.
What would make us cut further
  • Shriram Finance or L&T Finance giving back this quarter's NIM/growth gains without a stated reason.
  • A second consecutive quarter of Cholamandalam's insurance combined ratio above 120%.
  • Mahindra Finance's D/E rising further without a matching improvement in disclosed asset quality.

Caveats

  • PAT figures disagree across sources. Cholamandalam (₹1,789cr/+42% vs. ₹1,656cr/+46% cited elsewhere), L&T Finance (₹902cr/+29% vs. ₹916cr), Sundaram Finance (₹636.19cr/+33.9% vs. ₹522cr), Mahindra Finance (₹926.03cr vs. ₹899cr vs. ₹927cr) and IIFL Finance (₹467cr standalone vs. ₹675–713cr consolidated cited across outlets) — most likely standalone-vs-consolidated or pre/post-minority-interest differences that could not be fully reconciled from public reporting in the time available. Treat PAT growth rates as directionally right, not exact to the decimal.
  • This sector has no standard metric template. NIM/yield, AUM growth and PAT growth are derived from first principles, not a standing sector library entry. Mahindra Finance's scale metric is disbursement growth (+21% YoY) because AUM growth itself was not disclosed.
  • Cross-model comparability is limited. The universe spans a bank (CSB Bank, NIM ~3.7%), gold-loan NBFCs (Muthoot, Manappuram, IIFL — yields 16–18%) and vehicle/diversified NBFCs (NIM 8–9% where disclosed). A higher number on the league table is not "better" across business models.
  • Several figures are marked n/d rather than estimated. L&T Finance, Sundaram Finance and Mahindra Finance did not disclose NIM/GNPA/NNPA in the sources reviewed. Poonawalla Fincorp's and Mahindra Finance's true YoY AUM growth were not found (QoQ or disbursement growth used as the closest available proxy, labelled). Sundaram Finance's exact results date could not be pinned to one source.
  • Three growth rates are low-base artefacts. Poonawalla Fincorp (+391.5% PAT), Manappuram (+341% consolidated PAT) and IIFL Finance (+160% PAT) compare against a depressed Q1 FY26. Excluded from the AUM-vs-PAT scissor for that reason; the growth is real but the multiple is not a run-rate.
  • IndoStar Capital Finance was in the phase-1 universe (11 names) but was not part of the "1–10" selection and is not covered in this report.
  • The scissor, league table and pressure-point chart use only companies with clean, comparably-sourced figures for that specific metric. Exclusions are noted at each chart rather than filled with an estimate.
  • Walk-the-talk covers 4 of 10 companies — Shriram Finance, Cholamandalam, L&T Finance and CSB Bank — because a traceable prior-quarter or standing multi-year commitment could be sourced only for these four. Muthoot, Manappuram and IIFL set their relevant guidance this quarter rather than last; no explicit forward commitment was found for Sundaram Finance, Mahindra Finance or Poonawalla Fincorp in the material reviewed.
