08 / Red Flags & Accounting Forensics

Client Concentration in Specialized ER&D

Niche leaders like KPIT Tech depend on top 5 OEM clients for >50% of revenue, leaving performance exposed to client R&D budget shifts.

M&A Goodwill & Integration Overhead

Inorganic acquisitions (e.g. Coforge-Cigniti) carry integration costs and goodwill amortization that require ongoing monitoring.

US H-1B Visa & Offshoring Regulatory Friction

Tightening US immigration policies and onsite wage inflation mandate higher offshore engineering mix to protect EBIT margins.

10 / Sector Synthesis & Strategic Outlook

Indian IT Midcaps are outperforming tier-1 IT giants by specializing in high-growth niches: Software Product Engineering (Persistent), Travel & Insurance IT (Coforge), and Automotive ER&D (KPIT).

With offshore utilization expanding and GenAI production implementation deal wins accelerating, top-tier midcap IT stocks remain prime institutional compounders.
