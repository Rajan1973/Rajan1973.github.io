What management said

Key takeaways from Q1 FY27 corporate presentations and earnings calls.

Occupancy & RevPAR Recovery
  • ITC Hotels: Occupancy at 68%, RevPAR up 10% YoY. Business travel leading recovery.
  • Lemon Tree: Occupancy 62%, RevPAR growth 5% YoY. Focused on corporate segment.
  • EIH Ltd: Luxury segment occupancy 72%, strong ARR growth.
Cost Management
  • Employee cost inflation 8-12% YoY across sector, outpacing revenue growth.
  • ITC maintaining cost discipline through owned asset efficiency.
  • Lemon Tree implementing automation to offset labour cost pressure.
Asset Mix & Expansion
  • Owned vs managed property mix varies significantly across names.
  • ITC: 90% owned/operated properties. Stronger margin capture.
  • Others: Growing management fee income from franchise model.
  • New key additions across pipeline in tier-2 cities.

Caveats

  • RevPAR is the core unit economic — revenue per available room drives performance, not absolute revenue or profit.
  • Owned vs managed mix varies — ITC Hotels dominates with owned/operated properties; others rely on management fees.
  • Labour cost pressure is structural — 18-25% of revenue is industry norm; growth above this compresses margins.
  • Occupancy recovery phase — data reflects early recovery; seasonal patterns may vary QoQ.
