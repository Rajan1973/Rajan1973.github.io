08 / Red Flags & Accounting Forensics

Cotton Raw Material Price Volatility

Unseasonal monsoon shifts in domestic cotton growing belts can drive sudden raw cotton price spikes, squeezing non-integrated spinners.

US Retailer Demand Sensitivity

Exporters with >80% US exposure remain vulnerable to US consumer discretionary spending slowdowns or trade tariff revisions.

Working Capital Holding Days

Seasonal cotton purchasing requires large upfront working capital borrowing during Q3/Q4 harvest cycles.

10 / Sector Synthesis & Strategic Outlook

Indian Textiles & Apparel sector is entering a multi-year export expansion cycle supported by US restocking, Bangladesh order shift, and upcoming UK/EU FTAs.

Low-cost integrated players (KPR Mill, Gokaldas Exports, Welspun Living) represent high-conviction institutional picks.
