About This Site

What is Markets with Rajan?

A daily post-market intelligence report for active Indian equity traders and investors. Each report covers six sections of structured analysis published every trading day after market close:

  • §0 Executive Scorecard — indices, VIX, FII/DII, commodities
  • §1 Nifty Movement — OHLC, narrative, global cues
  • §2 Macro & Policy — RBI, SEBI, Fed, geopolitical events
  • §3 Breadth — 5-day A/D comparison, SMA%, sector rotation
  • §4 FII/DII Flows — daily, weekly, MTD trend analysis
  • §5 F&O Intelligence — PCR, max pain, OI strikes, expiry outlook
  • §6 F&O Expiry Intel — live Kite MCP futures OI pattern analysis

Data sourced from Zerodha Kite MCP (live F&O OI), NSE, MCX, and real-time web feeds. Not investment advice — for educational and research purposes only.
