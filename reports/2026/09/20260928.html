13e. The Critical Observation

Six of seven heavyweights show fresh short buildup once Sep+Oct OI is combined — a near-unanimous picture, with ICICI Bank's +36.9% combined OI rise the most extreme of the set. Infosys is the lone exception, showing genuine long buildup (price up, OI up) — a real, if isolated, bright spot rather than a positioning unwind. It is nothing of the sort — it's rollover, with the underlying short positioning growing, not shrinking. Combined with the index futures' own sharp OI buildup in §13c and a PCR sitting at 0.84 (call-side skew, consistent with resistance capping upside), the F&O tape agrees firmly with the cash-market Bearish regime read in §2.
