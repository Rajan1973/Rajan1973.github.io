What's Unusual Today

  • Nifty IT participation jumped 60 points in five sessions 1 of 24
    The largest single-sector rotation this report has recorded. The share of IT stocks above their 20-day average went from a small minority to a clear majority inside a week. IT was also the only sector to gain participation today while every other one lost it.
  • Vardhman Textiles +5.29% on 36.5× normal volume
    The most extreme volume reading in the entire universe by a wide margin — more than double the next highest. A move this size on that much volume is a crowd arriving in one session, not a drift.
  • Engineers India +6.94% on 15.7× volume
    Second-heaviest volume expansion of the day, and the top gainer among construction names.
  • Caplin Point +7.7% on 11.2× volume
    The day's largest gain, on the third-heaviest volume — and notable because healthcare was otherwise the day's second-worst sector at ‑1.60%.
  • Nifty ATR still 0.73% of price 41 of 250
    The average daily range has not widened at all, through an oil shock, a bank selloff and an expiry. Realised volatility remains near the calm end of 250 sessions while the market's internals deteriorate — a divergence worth watching rather than trusting.
  • Both FII and DII bought on the same session 28 of 36
    Foreign +₹1,143.38 Cr and domestic +₹1,846.94 Cr. After four sessions of the two pulling in opposite directions, they agreed today — though expiry-day mechanics make one session a weak signal.
  • Welspun Corp +6.8%, and in both momentum tables
    A name entering momentum today that is also already in a confirmed weekly trend. Only two stocks qualify for both.
  • KEI Industries ‑6.83%, Polycab ‑5.82%
    The two largest cable and wire names fell together and led the losers, a sector-specific move rather than part of the broad decline.
  • Graphite India +4.32% on 5.8× volume
    Part of a scattered bid in capital goods that ran counter to the day's direction.
  • Nifty 500 advance/decline at 0.64 86 of 246
    Better than yesterday's 0.56 but still two fallers for every three risers — on a day the index closed almost unchanged. The flat headline hides three consecutive sessions of negative breadth.

§8

Sector Rotation

Rotating in

  • Information Technology — participation up an extraordinary 60 points in five sessions, the largest rotation this report has recorded. IT rose 0.98% today while thirteen of fourteen sectors fell, and is up 2.36% on the week.
  • Private Banks and Pharma — each up 10 points of participation over the week, though both fell today. Improving from a low base rather than leading.

Rotating out

  • Auto — participation down 47 points in five sessions, the worst on the board, and ‑2.48% on the week. Maruti fell 4.41% today. This was already deteriorating yesterday and accelerated.
  • Realty — down 40 points, ‑2.89% on the week, ‑1.42% today. Second consecutive session as one of the worst.
  • Media — down 30 points and ‑3.02% on the week, extending a month-long decline with no base forming.
  • Oil & Gas — down 26 points despite rising crude, which is the counter-intuitive one: higher oil hurts refiners and marketers more than it helps producers in this index.
  • Midcaps broadly — Midcap 100 and Next 50 both shed 22 points of participation. The broader market is where the selling actually landed.
Yesterday's defensive rotation has narrowed to a single sector. On Monday, hospitals, pharma, healthcare and banks were all gaining participation. Today only IT is — and every other sector, including the defensives that led yesterday, lost ground. A rotation that narrows this fast is usually not a rotation; it is the market retreating to one hiding place.
