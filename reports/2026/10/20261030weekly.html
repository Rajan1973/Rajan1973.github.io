Key takeaways

  • Nifty rose 2.00% to 23,053.10 and won back 23,000.
  • Pharma (+7.09%) and Healthcare (+6.91%) led on strong results.
  • Power (+5.90%) bounced hard after last week's sell-off.
  • Cement (-1.79%) and Oil & Gas (-0.74%) were the only big sectors down.
  • FIIs were net buyers in cash for the week, and Brent ended near a six-month high.

01Benchmarks

  • Nifty 50: 22,600.00 to 23,053.10, up 453.10 points (+2.00%). Week range 22,387.35–23,118.10.
  • Sensex: 74,459.53, up 1,335.70 points (+1.83%).
  • Midcap 100: 55,800.65 to 57,301.25 (+2.69%). It fell 0.93% on Friday.
  • Smallcap 100: 17,739.45 to 18,463.60 (+4.08%). It fell 1.85% on Friday.
  • Nifty 500: +2.19%.
  • Nifty Next 50: +2.86%.
  • India VIX: 16.86 to 15.30 (-9.25%).
  • Breadth: 308 of 495 Nifty 500 stocks rose for the week (my count from the NSE bhavcopy).
  • The month: Nifty +1.9% and Sensex +2.7% in October.
    • That snapped a two-month losing streak.
    • Small-caps fell 4.2% and mid-caps 3.4% for the month.
  • Friday: the week was strong but the finish was weak.
    • Nifty rose on IT.
    • About 2,860 stocks fell against 1,313 that rose.

03Best sectors and why

Pharma (+7.09%) and Healthcare (+6.91%): results did the work.

  • Dr Reddy's: +9.96% for the week.
    • It rose 6.6% on Monday after its Q2 beat expectations.
  • Laurus Labs: +22.11%.
    • Q2 revenue grew 33%.
    • Its CDMO (contract manufacturing) business grew 86%.
    • ICICI Securities started coverage with a Buy and a ₹2,520 target.
  • Piramal Pharma: jumped more than 17% on Wednesday after results.
  • Acutaas: +27.60%.
  • PPL Pharma: +25.04%.
  • Mankind: +12.66%.
  • Zydus: +10.28%.
  • Divi's: +9.27%.
  • Sun Pharma: a laggard at +1.70%.
    • Reported Q2 profit fell 36% to ₹1,989 cr.
    • One-time charges tied to the Organon deal caused it.
    • Adjusted profit rose 1.4%. US sales fell 8.2%.
  • Apollo Hospitals: +9.29%. It rose 4.43% on Wednesday alone.
  • Max Healthcare: +5.52%.
  • The view: InvestValue's Siddharth Purohit tied the gains to good Q2 numbers. He also cited the sector's defensive appeal.

Power (+5.90%): a rebound after last week's sharp fall.

  • SJVN: +12.64%.
  • NHPC: +11.31%.
  • JSW Energy: +11.55%.
    • Q2 profit rose 38% to ₹1,141 cr.
    • Jefferies kept a Buy (₹720 target).
    • CLSA kept a Hold and cut its estimates.
  • Power Grid: +7.11%.
  • NTPC: +6.12%.
  • Tata Power: slipped 0.88%.
  • The view:
    • Purohit called last week's fall profit-booking, not weaker business.
    • Ventura's Vinit Bolinjkar called it bargain buying.

Retail (+4.68%)

  • Trent: +10.63%.
    • Q2 profit rose about 9%. Revenue rose 22.5%.
    • It was the top Nifty gainer on Friday.

Media (+4.34%)

  • The index jumped nearly 4% on Tuesday.
  • PVR INOX: +7.12%.
  • Sun TV: +4.97%.

Capital Markets (+4.01%)

  • Stocks rose on reports about capital gains tax and STT changes.
  • Friday's reports of a possible LTCG tax cut added to it.
  • BSE: +8.65%.

Realty (+3.77%)

  • Lodha: +7.68%.
  • DLF: +5.26%.
  • Godrej Properties: +4.78%.
  • Phoenix: +4.53%.
  • Sobha: fell 4.12%.
  • The view: Bolinjkar said realty deserves more caution. It's rate-sensitive.

Also worth noting.

  • IT: ended +1.91%, but it swung hard.
    • It fell about 2% on Wednesday on AI-earnings worries.
    • It led on Friday (+2.32%). Investors treated Indian IT as a refuge from the US Big Tech sell-off.
    • IT rose 17.7% in October. That's its best month in over six years.
    • Winners: LTTS +9.46%, Zensar +8.47%, Coforge +7.60%, Infosys +2.70%.
    • Losers: eClerx -17.71%, Hexaware -15.06%, Intellect -13.39%, TCS -1.08%.
  • Metal: +2.67%. It led on Monday on strong results.
    • JSW Steel +5.10%.
    • Vedanta +5.03%.
    • SAIL fell 2.03%.
  • Kotak Bank: +7.22% after a 26% Q2 profit rise.
  • L&T: +6.16%. It rose more than 4% on Wednesday after results.
  • Adani Ports: +8.65% after strong Q2 numbers.

05The macro drivers

  • Fed: held at 3.75%–4% on Wednesday.
    • Two members wanted a hike.
  • ECB: held. It debated a hike.
    • Lagarde will step down in February 2027.
  • BOJ: held.
  • Crude: Brent's December contract settled at $117.64 on Friday.
    • Up 13.6% in October and 0.79% for the week.
    • That's a fourth straight monthly gain.
    • A tanker attack in Hormuz drove Friday's jump.
  • US Big Tech:
    • Alphabet fell 12.6% on Thursday.
    • Amazon and Intel slid before Friday's US open.
    • That pushed money into Indian IT on Friday.
  • Rupee: closed Friday at 94.95 per dollar.
    • It was 30 paise stronger on the day.
  • Jio Platforms: listed at a 21.4% premium to its ₹1,119 issue price.
    • It became India's second most valuable company.
