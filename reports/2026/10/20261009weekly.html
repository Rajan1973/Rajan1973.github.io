Method and caveats

  • Universe. The 500 stocks in the Nifty 500 membership snapshot dated 2026-08-14 in the index_membership table of your Supabase project. Constituent changes after that date are not reflected.
  • Returns. Weekly returns are close-to-close, price only, from daily prices: 1 Oct close to 9 Oct close (4 sessions; 2 Oct was an exchange holiday). The previous week is 24 Sep close to 1 Oct close (5 sessions). Index returns are recomputed from closes; "% below 52w high" is measured against the 52-week intraday high.
  • Checks. Start and end closes for all 65 of the 65 listed stocks matched NSE Bhavcopy closes. Corporate-action lookups for 25 Sep to 9 Oct returned no split, bonus, rights or dividend ex-dates for 65 of 65, so no corporate-action artefacts were found and adjusted returns equal raw returns.
  • Catalysts. From web search, the research filings tables (no rows dated inside the window; latest entries are from August and early September 2026) and the Tijori knowledge base (periodic filings only). News coverage of 5 to 9 Oct 2026 was thin, and several large outlets could not be reached by the search tool. “No stock-specific catalyst found” means none was found, not that none exists. Tapetide was not used (daily quota).
  • Confidence. High: a dated report ties stock-specific news to the move. Medium: a dated source names the event or sector driver and the stock moved that day, but the link is partly inferred. Low: no dated stock-specific source; the stated driver is inference.
  • Relative volume. Average daily volume over 5 to 9 Oct divided by a baseline average. Winners: baseline is the 16 sessions before the week (9 Sep to 1 Oct). Losers: baseline is the 21-session NSE average (9 Sep to 9 Oct, which includes the week), so earlier spike days can inflate the baseline and understate activity (noted for BBTC, HBLENGINE, TEGA, ELECON, WHIRLPOOL, RITES, IRCON).
  • Base effect. Tagged where the catalyst notes say the 1 Oct base close followed a spike day, so part of the weekly fall reverses that spike: SCHNEIDER, ENRIN, WELSPUNLIV.
