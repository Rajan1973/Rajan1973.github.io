Key takeaways

  • Third straight weekly gain for the benchmark. Mid and small caps beat it.
  • PSU Bank led by a wide margin. Media and Capital Markets followed.
  • IT was the worst, snapping a six-week winning run. Cement and Commodities also fell.
  • October CPI rose to 6.09% from 5.54%, above the RBI’s 6% upper band.
  • Moody’s raised India’s FY27 GDP forecast to 7% from 6.5%. Iran de-escalation hopes and softer crude helped Wednesday. A weaker rupee weighed on Friday.

04 Worst sectors

IT

  • It fell on all four days. It snapped a six-week winning run.
  • Global AI and chip stocks were in turmoil.
  • On Monday, 10 big IT names lost about ₹50,200 cr in market value.
  • Anand Rathi says investors will separate firms that earn from enterprise AI from those spending heavily to build it.
  • Nvidia named CFO Colette Kress interim CEO after Jensen Huang resigned. Its shares fell 4.3% on Thursday.
  • Choice Institutional Equities: tier-2 IT grew about 5.4% QoQ in Q2 vs about 1.1% for tier-1.

07 Data caveats

  • Index and stock % changes are from NSE closing files for 6 Nov and 13 Nov.
  • Some weekly reviews do not match those closes. A VIX of 16.30 was quoted against the NSE close of 15.50.
  • A Nifty range of 25,054–25,299 looks like closing levels rather than the week’s intraday range.
  • Some outlets reported PSU Bank “over 3%”. NSE closes show +7.50%.
  • Some outlets put the cement index up 0.27%. NSE closes show −2.08%.
  • Some outlets list ONGC as a +2.9% gainer. The NSE bhavcopy shows a 2.92% fall.
  • Figures that clash with NSE closes were not used for index or stock changes.
