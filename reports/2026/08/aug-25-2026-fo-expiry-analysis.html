EXPIRY WEEK PATTERN RECOGNITION — Historical Context: The specific pattern seen here — 5-day short buildup followed by a violent 2-day short cover just before the monthly expiry — is one of the most consistently recurring patterns in Nifty monthly series expiries. This setup has historically preceded one of three outcomes: (1) A "short squeeze expiry" where the market closes 200–400 pts above max pain; (2) A "max pain pin" where the squeeze reverses into Monday and the market closes right at max pain; (3) A "bear trap completion" where the short covering was the setup, and after covering, the market falls through to 23,900–24,000 into Tuesday. The data currently points most strongly to scenario (1) or (2) — the squeeze already started, and max pain is only 102 pts below spot.
